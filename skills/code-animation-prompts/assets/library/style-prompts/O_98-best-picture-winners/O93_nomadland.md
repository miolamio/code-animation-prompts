# O93 Nomadland

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Here and Now 2020–2025
- Looks like: Powdery pastel strokes on grey-violet paper, a wide plain and an old camper van, the sky shifting from golden yellow to rose to violet. Nomadland lives on that long, empty horizon at dusk, and pastel makes it quiet and soft.
- Style: magic hour pastel drawing
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 2020

## Style prompt

Soft pastel on grey-violet toned paper #7d7079, tooth showing everywhere as fine speckle (±8% brightness). Sky blended smooth, a few long thin cloud bands. The land carries the texture: dry-grass ochre (gold dulled toward the paper grey) under fine upright grass strokes (1/400 of frame width wide, 1/40 long, ±15% value), dense in the foreground, thinning toward the horizon. Objects get soft diagonal hatching, broken edges. Palette by role: gold #f6cf7a at the horizon, rose #c9738a in the lower sky, violet #4b3f78 for high sky, clouds and far land, dusk blue #2b2d5c for shadows. A long horizon near mid-frame over open land or water; the subject mid-sized in the lower half. The sky drifts gold to rose to violet; lamps brighten as it darkens. One slow pan with a light push-in. Avoid: oily impasto, sharp edges, bare smooth ground.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Soft pastel on colored paper, magic-hour dusk palette, paper grain texture

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Fern, with no props, only three actions: walking, sitting, looking. One take with no cut: the walk slowly comes to a stop at a blanket, she sits down, then lifts her eyes to the horizon; the sky darkens as the camp lamp and the light at the van door grow warmer.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Wide shot, single continuous take: Clawd walks past an old white camper van with its side door open, the camera tracking sideways with her and pushing in gently as the plain and a distant lone hill slide slowly by; she sits down on a striped blanket by a camp lamp and looks toward the horizon as the sky goes from golden yellow to violet. (2.5s)
