## Style prompt


---

English:

Real-time 3D, one unbroken handheld long take, after cinematographer Emmanuel Lubezki (*Birdman*, 2014). A narrow, lived-in passage (default a backstage corridor; an outdoor subject gets its nearest interior, e.g. a stairwell): chipped blood-red enamel dado #6e2a22 on the lower third, stained nicotine plaster #cdb98e above, pipes, cable trays, doors, pinned notices. Light only from practicals every 2–3 m: warm caged tungsten bulbs, one cold flickering fluorescent tube, a red exit sign #ff2a1a; the far door glows. Mid-key: plaster light near lamps, never below mid-grey between them; floor visible. Wide lens (18–24 mm), slow sway (±1°), soft ambient occlusion, light bloom, fine grain (±3%). Over 20–40 s, in any order: lead the subject walking backwards, whip aside to a doorway, let it brush past the lens, turn and follow. Avoid: a locked-off camera, blank walls, repeated textures, a crushed dark frame.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Real-time 3D (three.js), Lubezki handheld long take, tungsten and fluorescent lights, fine digital-cinema grain

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Riggan, in a slate-blue Birdman helmet with a gold beak and wings on both sides. The corridor is a fast handheld camera move with the drummer's swinging drum beat in the frame; the push on the back door lands on the beat, cutting to Times Square, where Clawd stops and looks up.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Handheld medium shot, the camera walking backward in front of Clawd in the Birdman helmet, whipping left to the drummer playing a jazz drum kit in a small room, then whipping back as Clawd walks past the drum kit, brushing by the lens. (1.3s)
2. Cut to the back door, shot from behind Clawd with a red EXIT light overhead; the camera drifts gently sideways as Clawd shoves the door open onto a wash of white light. (0.7s)
3. Cut to Times Square at night: wide shot, Clawd standing on the sidewalk with a wall of LED screens behind him; he takes a step and looks up at the screens, the handheld camera drifting gently. (0.5s)
