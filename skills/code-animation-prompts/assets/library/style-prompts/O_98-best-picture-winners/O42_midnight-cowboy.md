# O42 Midnight Cowboy

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · The Swinging Sixties 1960–1969
- Looks like: Warhol's photo silkscreen: a black halftone photograph layer printed unevenly, pressed out of register over loose flat color blocks on a silver ground, the image repeated two and three times. The film has a Warhol-Factory-style party, and his Elvis was exactly a cowboy.

## Style prompt


---

English:

Andy Warhol photo-silkscreen. Two plates: a high-contrast black halftone photograph at 45°, dots about 1/200 of frame width, few mid-tones; and hand-painted flat colour blocks underneath, offset about 1/100 of frame width with wobbly edges: taxi yellow #F3BE14, skin pink #F2A197, cream #EFE6CF, red #E2462B, on brushed silver paint #BABCC0 with a soft sheen. The one hero subject takes the strongest block colour. Uneven squeegee pressure: dots flood into blotches in places, drag into dry horizontal streaks and pinholes elsewhere, over faint canvas weave. Repetition is the composition: the same figure or object, full-length, printed side by side two then three times, slightly overlapping, on empty silver. Settings appear only as drained-to-silver prints in the repeat. Camera: handheld shake, a push in, flat frontal tableaux. Reference: Andy Warhol, *Double Elvis* (1963). Avoid: clean even halftone, perfect registration, uniform crispness.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

photo silkscreen, Warhol's *Double Elvis* (1963), silver-paint ground, misregistered color blocks

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Joe Buck: a black Stetson, a red neckerchief, suede fringe on the arms. First it performs the line from the film, "I'm walkin' here!": a taxi brakes hard, it jumps back, and slaps the hood shouting. Then the picture turns into a Warhol silkscreen, with Clawd striking Elvis's gunslinger stance, printed twice and then three times.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. A crosswalk on Sixth Avenue: a yellow taxi brakes hard and screeches in, Clawd (Joe Buck) jumps back with wide eyes and slaps the hood; handheld shake, slow push-in. (1.1s)
2. Close shot of Joe Buck: a cigarette in his mouth, yelling at the taxi and pointing at the driver (I'm walkin' here!); push in. (0.6s)
3. The street scene fades to silver and is printed as a Warhol double: two Clawds in Stetsons, revolvers in hand, overlapping, then becoming three; a frontal eye-level freeze frame. (0.8s)
