# O14 How Green Was My Valley

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Color and War 1939–1949
- Looks like: opaque gouache in the gold-and-green palette of the 1941 original poster: solid color blocks, coal-black slag heaps, heavy dark Kyffin Williams-style mountain masses and broken outlines. Gouache rather than woodcut sets it apart from Part 3; what it paints is miners singing as they walk down the valley toward home.
- Style: Welsh valley gouache
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1941

## Style prompt


---

English:

Opaque gouache after Welsh painter Kyffin Williams. Directional strokes 1/80-1/40 of frame width, 2-5 times as long, ragged, fully covering; broken dark dashes outline buildings, walls and ridges; dark hills flecked with lighter green or slate knife marks. Palette: sky and light pale gold #F1C565 (not mustard), fields green #86A03F, darks coal #1D1B1E, far hills and water slate #48505E, small accents yellow #F6CF2F, other colours mixed from these; the hero the story centres on #D97757, at least 1/8 of frame height. Fields and sky light (~60%), hills and buildings dark. Warm grade under 10%, vignette, grain. Default low late-afternoon side light: fields lit, ridges rim-lit. From high ground in true perspective, a path leading the eye down; small repeated buildings (or walls, rocks) stepping along slopes. Motion: looping smoke or cloud, a lateral track. Avoid: watercolour transparency, vector fills, mud.

## Notes

opaque gouache, Kyffin Williams palette-knife landscape, 1941 original poster palette

### Director's notes

Clawd plays young Huw: a tweed flat cap, a knotted red scarf, a daffodil. First he sits on the hilltop looking over the valley, then falls in step with the singing miners marching down the street. Two hard cuts: valley → pithead wheel → street.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Looking down from a hilltop among daffodils: Clawd in a flat cap and red scarf sits in the left foreground holding a daffodil, the row-house street running all the way down to the pithead and slag heap in the valley floor; slow pull back. (1s)
2. Close-up of the pithead frame and its turning big wheel, row houses crowding the green slope behind; fixed camera. (0.5s)
3. A lateral tracking shot along the row-house street: miners coming off shift walk down in three ranks, coal dust on their faces, singing as they go; Clawd walks in the front rank, the houses sliding past behind him. (1s)
