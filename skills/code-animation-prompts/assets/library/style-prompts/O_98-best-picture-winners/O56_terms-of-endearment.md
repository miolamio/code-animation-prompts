# O56 Terms of Endearment

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Blockbuster Era 1980–1989
- Looks like: A family photo album lying open, filled with faded Polaroids and Kodak snapshots: greenish blacks, cream highlights, white borders. One of the Polaroids comes alive as a panning shot of a beach car chase. Terms of Endearment spans thirty years of mother and daughter.
- Style: family album Polaroids
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1983

## Style prompt

1970s–80s snapshots in an open album. Faded dyes: blacks lifted to teal grey about #2F3A3A, creamy rolled-off highlights, mild warm cast, slight overexposure, softness, fine grain (±4%), saturation about 60% of normal. White Polaroid borders, photo-booth strips (four frames stacked), ballpoint captions in the user's language. The album fills the frame, cream pages seen from above in soft, even light, a faint plastic sheen. In a photo, a moving subject gets a panning-shutter look (background smeared sideways, subject sharp); still subjects get shallow depth of field. People look photographed: real proportions, soft shading, slight blur, small faces. Camera: an overhead push into one photo until it fills the frame (about 3 s), a cut into the living scene as colour returns, then a pull-back. References: Polaroid SX-70, Kodacolor prints. Avoid: flat cartoon shapes, crisp modern colour, light shafts, saturated prints.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

SX-70 and Polaroid 600 prints, Kodak color snapshots, 1970s-80s magnetic photo album, panning-shot motion streaks

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Garrett, a retired astronaut in gold aviator sunglasses, with a laughing Aurora sitting next to him. Overall about 1.8x speed. After the push-in on the album it cuts straight into the photo, the faded tones warming up into fresh color; as the color returns he raises both arms, and the final close shot lands on the open-armed pose.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. High-angle shot of an open album on a walnut table, pushing all the way in on the loose Galveston Polaroid until the photo fills the picture. (1.2s)
2. The Polaroid comes alive: a red Corvette races through the surf, Aurora in the passenger seat waving, the color slowly returning; the camera pulls back to a wider beach. (0.8s)
3. Close shot: Clawd as Garrett in gold aviator sunglasses stands up behind the seat and spreads his arms; slow push-in. (0.5s)
