# O31 Gigi

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Widescreen 1950–1959
- Looks like: Toulouse-Lautrec-style chromolithograph: large flat areas, dry-brush contours, spattered ink dots in place of halftone, caricature-like faces. Gigi's moment is walking into Maxim's with all of Paris turning to stare at her, exactly the Paris Lautrec painted.
- Style: Lautrec Belle Epoque lithograph
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1958

## Style prompt


---

English:

Belle Époque colour lithograph after Toulouse-Lautrec. Flat colour plates with wobbly edges; figures and main shapes outlined in a loaded brush (blunt start, swelling middle, split dry tail), about 1/250 of frame width. Crachis only on shadow sides, cast shadows and glows: 1–3 px dots at 1920×1080, sparse, under 15% of the frame, a darker step of the colour beneath. Large surfaces get grouped dry-brush streaks. Palette: yellow #F1C232, orange-red #E0602C, olive #6E7036, black #1D1A16 on cream stone-grained paper #EFE3C3, plus a muted bottle teal and greyed mauve; flesh is orange-red thinned with paper. Caricatured profiles with drawn faces; one large figure cropped by the frame edge in front. Flat, even light; shadows are flat plates. Camera: shallow parallax, a slow pan settling on a medium shot. Avoid: Mucha halos, smooth gradients, regular halftone, all-over speckle.

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Gaston: a top hat, a white bow tie, a cane, with Gigi on its arm. The two keep walking inward while the eyes of the whole room turn with them. Three cuts land on the beat: a wide shot of the entrance, a pan across the staring crowd, and back to the pair, ending on Gigi winking at the camera.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Wide shot of Maxim's: Clawd (Gaston) in a top hat and white bow tie walks in with Gigi on his arm, cropped foreground silhouettes on either side; the nearest tables of guests begin to turn their heads; slow push-in with a drift to the right. (0.8s)
2. A pan along the red velvet banquettes: caricature faces turn one after another to stare, lorgnettes raised and fans frozen. (0.7s)
3. Medium shot of the two continuing inward; Gigi's gaze turns to the camera and she winks, just like the original poster; slight move to the right. (1s)
