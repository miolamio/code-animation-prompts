# O82 The Hurt Locker

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · New Millennium 2000–2009
- Looks like: A page from a US Army bomb disposal manual: even black lines, hatched shadows, stippled sand and soil, circled numbers and leader lines, an exploded IED diagram, with just a touch of olive green and sand. The Hurt Locker is about procedure under pressure, and the manual diagram is that procedure drawn out.
- Style: bomb disposal field manual line drawing
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 2009

## Style prompt

Military technical-manual illustration. Warm off-white paper #EFEADB with a faint engineering grid, uniform black ink #1B1A16 (outlines ~1/700 of frame width, hatching thinner), shading only by hatching, ground by stippling, flat tints of olive #6C6D42 and sand #D9C79C on 20–30% of the drawing; lights as ray lines, not glows. Main figure: a one-point-perspective view of the subject's setting filling most of the page; around it exploded views of the subject's key object along a dash-dot axis, circled numbers on leader lines, a parts table, WARNING and CAUTION boxes, typewriter labels (user's language) and FIG numbers, a black chapter card. Animate on twos: parts slide apart, a tool works, a figure walks. Camera: whole page, then cuts to enlarged insets. References: US Army technical manuals; Kathryn Bigelow, *The Hurt Locker* (2008). Avoid: gradient shading; too much colour; decorative fonts; sparse pages.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Military technical manual line drawings, exploded views, typewriter-font annotations, flat olive green and sand

### Director's notes

Clawd wears an olive green bomb disposal helmet with the visor raised, holds wire cutters, and stands among six half-buried 155mm shells. Four shots tighten from the full page down to the cut: the long walk, the face in the visor, Clawd's eyes, the wire cut. The snap lands near the end of the sequence.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. The full manual page, FIG 4-6 "THE LONG WALK": a soldier in a bomb suit walks toward the camera down a Baghdad dirt road, with Clawd standing among the daisy-chained shells holding wire cutters; the camera slowly pushes toward the street. (0.8s)
2. Close shot: the soldier in the bomb suit walking, a frowning face behind the visor; slow push-in. (0.6s)
3. Close shot: Clawd in the bomb disposal helmet with the visor raised, eyes looking down at the firing wire, the wire cutters raised; slight push-in. (0.5s)
4. Close-up: the wire cutters bite the twisted-pair firing wire above the shell, a squeeze, snap, and the wire parts; push in. (0.6s)
