## Notes

### Technique keywords

three.js 3D city, 1970s 35mm film grading, Steadicam tracking and circling rise

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Rocky: a gray hooded sweatshirt with the hood up, a white towel over its shoulders. The training montage is three quick cuts at about 1.7x speed, returning to 1x once it reaches the steps. A hard cut into the Steadicam run, with no more cuts after that: it runs to the top straight into the circling rise, and the arms-raised jump lands just as the camera begins to rise.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Medium shot in the meat locker, fixed camera: Clawd in a gray hood punches hanging beef, white tile, a bare bulb. (0.4s)
2. Closer, fixed camera: the fist hits the beef and the carcass swings. (0.4s)
3. A pre-dawn Italian market, fixed camera: Clawd jogs toward the camera past oil-drum fires, fruit and vegetable stalls and street lamps still lit. (0.6s)
4. A low-camera Steadicam chasing Clawd from behind as it runs up the 72 steps, the museum's columns ahead. (1.5s)
5. At the top, Clawd jumps with both arms up in a V; the camera circles it, rises and pulls back until the whole avenue and the city spread out in the sunrise. (2.1s)
