# O6 Cavalcade

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Silent and Early Sound 1927–1938
- Looks like: a Mucha-style Art Nouveau decorative painting: swollen brown contour lines, pastel flats, a mosaic ground, and a halo behind the central figure. The film opens on a family raising glasses to the new century on New Year's Eve 1899, so the halo is made into a clock face striking midnight.
- Style: Mucha Art Nouveau decorative painting
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1932/33

## Style prompt

Alphonse Mucha colour lithograph. Brown contours #4B3326, about 1/250 of frame width, doubling on the shadow side (light from upper left), tapered ends; pastel flats, each with a hard-edged darker shadow tone. Palette: cream #F5ECD6 paper/sky, sage #A9B490 foliage/drapery, pale gold #CFA55A halo/ornament, rose #DBA49C skin/flowers; the hero the story centres on in one stronger accent (e.g. #D97757). Wide frame: figure and a big ring-filled halo in the middle third, arched panels either side, behind them a muted-teal (sage plus grey) square-tile mosaic; whiplash vines, local flowers; a bottom band of serif capitals in the user's language, 1/25 of frame height. No plain area over 1/8 of frame. Almond eyes, heavy lids; hair or cloth in three-tone strands. Litho grain, paper mottle, vignette. Slow sway; tilt down to the halo, pull back. Avoid: thin clean lines, saturated colour, empty backgrounds.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Alphonse Mucha, chromolithograph, Art Nouveau ornament

### Director's notes

Clawd is a member of the Marryot family raising a toast: silk top hat, white bow tie, holding a champagne saucer. On beat 3 the clock strikes twelve and the halo flashes, then it cuts straight to the toast, where Clawd hops and lifts his glass. Played at 1:1, with two hard cuts; the final pull back holds on the whole poster.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Pan down from the CAVALCADE title and a galloping-horse frieze to the clock-face halo and push in; the hands close on XII and the halo flashes as the clock strikes. (1.1s)
2. Medium shot of the toast: Clawd in a top hat and white bow tie hops and raises his champagne saucer, with Jane and Robert raising their glasses on either side; gentle pull back. (0.8s)
3. Pull back to the whole Mucha decorative painting: three glasses held up, rose petals falling, and an inscription band at the bottom reading LONDON · XXXI DECEMBER MDCCCXCIX · A NEW CENTURY. (0.6s)
