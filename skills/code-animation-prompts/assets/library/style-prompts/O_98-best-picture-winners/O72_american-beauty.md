# O72 American Beauty

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Turn of the Century 1990–1999
- Looks like: Two kinds of picture cut together: first a clean, saturated, frontal and symmetrical suburban house (red door, red roses), then a cut to low-resolution DV footage with a REC interface. It matches Lester's respectable suburbs in the film and the plastic bag footage Ricky shoots.
- Style: symmetrical suburban cinema frame + DV footage
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1999

## Style prompt


---

English:

Two registers. Primary, about 70%: a clean, saturated illustrated frame, dead-on symmetrical, the hero centred before its home or setting; every shape softly modelled in 2–3 tones, dense detail (shrubs, flowers, foliage of many small leaf clusters). Default bright daylight; under other light stay bright (gold at sunset), never dim. Red accents like a door #C3141F, white trim, saturated greens, orange-red autumn trees; warm grade, light vignette and grain. Transition: curled red petals or leaves (about 1/60 of frame width) thicken into a 1-second blizzard across the lens, hiding the cut. Second register, about 30%: the same world as 1990s DV home video: soft, low-res, chroma bleed, macroblocks, interlace, noise, breathing exposure, greenish shadows, REC/battery/date OSD. One register per frame. Slow push-ins. Reference: cinematographer Conrad L. Hall (*American Beauty*, 1999). Avoid: video artefacts in the clean part; real brands' OSD; flat vector shapes.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Conrad Hall-style frontal symmetrical composition, rose petal fantasy, 1990s DV camcorder footage

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Lester: a white shirt with pointed collar tips, a navy tie with burgundy diagonal stripes, a red rose in hand. One push-in goes from the house to a close shot, with the petals growing denser all the way; at 1.3 seconds the cut to the footage is hidden in petals sweeping across the lens, so the fantasy flows straight into Ricky's videotape with no visible edit point.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Frontal symmetrical wide shot of the Burnham house, no cut, pushing straight in to Clawd on the sidewalk holding a long-stemmed red rose; the petals fall thicker and thicker until they sweep across the lens in sheets. (1.3s)
2. Ricky's camcorder picture (REC, OCT. 17 1998, 12:07:33): a white plastic bag and dead leaves swirling in front of a red brick wall; slow push-in, drifting right. No Clawd. (1.2s)
