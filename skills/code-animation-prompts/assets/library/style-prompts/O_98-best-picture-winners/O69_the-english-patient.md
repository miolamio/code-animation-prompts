# O69 The English Patient

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Turn of the Century 1990–1999
- Looks like: Watercolor sketches pasted into an old book, tucked with maps, photographs, notes and pressed flowers, the whole thing in the poster's amber brown. Almasy's Herodotus stuffed with drawings and notes is the frame of the whole film, and the camera passes through one watercolor into the Cave of Swimmers.
- Style: explorer's scrapbook watercolor
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1996

## Style prompt


---

English:

A traveller's scrapbook, crowded edge to edge with 6–10 overlapping items, some running off frame: a taped watercolour (the main image, about 40% of frame), a hand-drawn map, a sepia photo, printed pages, handwritten notes, a pressed flower; each softly shadowed, with yellowed tape. The watercolour: wet-in-wet washes, warm ochre lights, hard-edged violet-rose shadows, pen contours, pooled edges, clear value contrast. Palette: amber paper #E8D6B0, sepia #6B4A2E, ochre #D08A3C, rose-violet #9C6A86, ink #2A1E16. Inside the painting: one warm source (torch, lamp) pooling in a dark foreground, bright sky beyond. Light amber grade on the page only, never muddying the painting; vignette about 20%, faint grain. Signature move: push into the painting until the page edges slide out. Reference: cinematographer John Seale (*The English Patient*, 1996). Avoid: smooth gradients; sparse tidy layouts; panning while a page frames the painting.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Transparent watercolor, pen notes and handwriting, scrapbook collage: tape, photographs, maps, amber brown palette

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Almasy: a linen desert headscarf with the tail fluttering, a Herodotus in one hand and a torch in the other. The camera pushing into the painting is itself the transition; after that, two hard cuts stay inside the cave, first the mural, then Clawd. Scene time is about 1.8x screen time.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. The scrapbook lies open on a table, and a push-in goes into the watercolor until the pages slide out past both edges of the frame: Clawd stands holding a torch in the painted Cave of Swimmers. (1.2s)
2. Hard cut to a lateral move sweeping across the swimmers mural on the cave wall, the firelight flickering; Clawd is not in the frame. (0.6s)
3. Hard cut to Clawd standing at left with a torch in one hand and a book in the other, the mural beside him and sand dunes beyond the cave mouth; slow camera move backward. (0.7s)
