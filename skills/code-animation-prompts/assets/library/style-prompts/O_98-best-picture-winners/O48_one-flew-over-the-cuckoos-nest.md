# O48 One Flew Over the Cuckoo's Nest

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · New Hollywood 1970–1979
- Looks like: a quiet realism in the manner of Edward Hopper: large flat pale green walls, hard-edged parallelograms of low morning light, people kept apart from one another. This suits the ward in *One Flew Over the Cuckoo's Nest*: order under fluorescent light, broken by one escape.
- Style: Hopper-style American realist oil painting
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1975

## Style prompt

Edward Hopper-style American realist painting. Large flat planes of restrained, pale colour: institutional greens #9EB8A0 and #5E7C66, grey-green floor #7E8876, cream #ECE6D2, on visible canvas (±3%), with a few long dragged strokes about 1/80 of frame width, 5–10% off the base. Low side sun throws hard-edged parallelograms of warm light #FCE6AC on walls and ground, crossed by crisp shadows of frames or railings, indoors or out. Sky pale cream-green; skin warmed cream; clothes darkened greens. Figures painted from 3–4 overlapping soft-edged value shapes, no outline, real proportions; several people stand apart. Stillness, then one sudden event under a second: a whip pan, a burst of light. Signature shot: a still one-point interior, light on the floor. References: Edward Hopper, *Office in a Small City* (1953). Avoid: heavy outlines, vector figures, dark saturated walls, fuzzy light patches.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Edward Hopper-style realism, hard-edged raking window light, flat oil color blocks with dragged strokes

### Director's notes

Clawd wears McMurphy's black knit cap and plaid collar, and plays the moment near the end: smashing the hydrotherapy console out through the window. Screen time equals scene time: crouch and strain, lift, throw at 1.0 s, hit the window at 1.3 s, the flash and flying glass coming toward the camera, then a cheer. Use whip pans with motion blur in place of cuts.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. One continuous take in the daytime dayroom: Clawd lifts the marble hydrotherapy console over its head and throws it, the camera whip-panning with it to the rear window, the glass bursting and morning light flooding in, then whipping back through the light to Clawd, who raises both arms in a cheer. (2.5s)
