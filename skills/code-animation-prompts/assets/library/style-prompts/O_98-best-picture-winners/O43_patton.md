# O43 Patton

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · New Hollywood 1970–1979
- Looks like: a lithograph look imitating 1940s US war bond posters: graded flat folds, dark contours, halftone shadows, yellowed paper. It matches the opening of *Patton*: the small general addressing the troops before a giant flag filling the stage.
- Style: WWII war bond lithograph poster
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1970

## Style prompt

1940s US war-bond lithograph poster. Flat inks: red #C2342C, navy #213261, olive #5F5C31, khaki #B7975E, on fibrous, foxed cream #E6D5B3 paper. Three values per form (bare paper, flat ink, ink plus halftone), posterised in hard steps. Brown-black key line about 1/500 of frame width, printed last; shadows as coarse 45° halftone bands (dots about 1/150 of frame width) with crayon grain; colour plates offset about 1/600 of frame width. Default: a symmetrical frontal stage, a small subject before a huge graphic shape filling most of the frame (flag, emblem, sunburst), lights as hard pale shapes or bare paper; outdoors too, light falling as a hard pale pool. Lettering optional, in the user's language. Camera: slow push-ins, one crisp pull-back. Reference: US war-bond posters, 1942–45. Avoid: airbrushed gradients, perfect registration, halftone so fine it reads grey. The paper has worn edges.

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Patton: a four-star helmet, ribbons and medals, an ivory-handled revolver, a riding crop. It walks into place beneath the stripes, snaps a salute when we cut to the close shot, holds, lowers it, slaps the crop into its palm and nods, as if the address is starting. Two hard cuts, and the final pull-out shrinks it to a small dot before the giant flag.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Red and white stripes fill the frame (a graphic match with the Warhol grid of the previous part), then a pull-out reveals Clawd walking up from the bottom of the frame to stand before the giant flag. (1s)
2. Medium close shot, slow push-in with a slight shake: Clawd under the four-star helmet snaps a salute, holds, lowers it, slaps the riding crop into its palm and gives a firm nod. (0.9s)
3. Wide shot: the camera pulls out past the flag to see the whole empty theater: red curtains, a gilded proscenium, footlights, with Clawd shrunk to a small dot at center stage. (0.5s)
