# O92 Parasite

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Indie Era 2010–2019
- Looks like: An architectural section cut through a Seoul hillside: a glass mansion on top, a secret bunker underground, and a route running all the way down to the flooded semi-basement, in fine lines and soft material colors. Parasite is built on exactly this vertical class divide, and one drawing holds the whole film.
- Style: architectural section axonometric
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 2019

## Style prompt

Vector section axonometric, low dimetric view: the cut face lies along the near-flat 7° axis, facing us, depth along the 42° axis at half scale; roof and near walls cut away to show the rooms. Cut through the subject's place (a house, a tower, a ship, a hillside). Poché on cut faces: dark concrete with aggregate, earth hatched at 45° with stones. 1 px dark-grey lines at every zoom. Default daylight under a pale sky: concrete #b3ada2, oak #d6b58a, charcoal #3e4146, pine #3f6b3e, white walls, green lawn, translucent blue water. Furnished rooms, simple scale figures. Night or other low light is a variation: multiply 30–40% moonlit blue, lit rooms painted back warm. Crane slowly framing 2–3 rooms along one continuous path; end on the whole drawing at ~80% of the frame. Avoid: 30° isometric, thick outlines, cuts between levels, a small drawing.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Isometric section axonometric, architectural cut-fill with scale figures, rainy-night grade

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Ki-woo, never letting go of the scholar's rock: holding it overhead on the lawn, hiding by the coffee table on the rainstorm night, then carrying it on his head all the way down the hill. Only two cuts in the whole piece; in every shot the camera travels up and down the section, so rich and poor share the same frame, and the 12 beats end on the full section.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. The glass mansion in section by day and a party on the striped lawn: Clawd stands at the garden gate and lifts the scholar's rock overhead with both hands; the camera slowly pushes in. (0.8s)
2. Cut to the rainstorm night: the Parks' living room, Clawd hiding by the coffee table; the camera drops like an elevator through the floor slab and the basement, landing in the bunker where the light blinks in Morse code. (1.3s)
3. Cut to a long take descending through the rain: the camera follows Clawd with the scholar's rock on his head from the front gate down flight after flight of long stairs, through a tunnel under the road, into the low alleys and the semi-basement now flooding, then rises and pulls back into the full section, the mansion at the top and the flooding at the very bottom. (2.9s)
