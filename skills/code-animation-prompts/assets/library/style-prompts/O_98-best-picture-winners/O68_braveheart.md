English:

An illuminated manuscript page (cropped, or an open spread) filling the frame. Vellum #ECDCB4, brown ink outlines #2A1A10, lapis #27469A, vermilion #C43A20, burnished gold leaf on raised gesso, with punched dot rosettes; a slow candle glint sweeps the gold only. Miniature backgrounds are mostly gold (night: lapis with gold stars); gold fills at least a tenth of the frame. Gothic figures in flat colour and ink outline. Celtic interlace, ivy borders, marginal grotesques; the story's text (Latin or the user's language) in broad-nib textura, red-and-blue line fillers, one large gilded initial. Layout: the miniature a window about half the page beside the text column, layered for parallax on pushes; figures may break out. Warm candle from upper left, brown vignette, pigment grain. References: Book of Kells, Morgan Bible, Luttrell Psalter. Avoid: flat yellow for gold; modern typefaces; glints on faces.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Book of Kells interlace, Gothic miniatures (Morgan Bible), Luttrell Psalter marginalia, burnished gold leaf

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Wallace: a two-handed greatsword, a tartan plaid sash, a stripe of blue woad on the forehead. Four hard cuts: initial, full page, bridge, clash. The charge grows fiercer through the middle, and in the last shot the scroll above his head unfurls to read freedom. Scene time is about 1.8x screen time.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Close-up of the gilded initial F, with Wallace inside its counter, half his face painted with blue woad, a streak of golden light sweeping across the gold leaf. (0.7s)
2. Hard cut to the full page: REEDOM, the blackletter body text, the margins, and in the middle a miniature of Stirling Bridge with Clawd charging at the front; gentle push-in. (0.6s)
3. Hard cut into the miniature: English soldiers crowd across a wooden bridge, a knight in a checkered horse cloth charges in, people struggling in the river. (0.6s)
4. Hard cut to a close shot of Clawd: he raises the two-handed greatsword to meet the knight's lance, and the speech scroll above his head unfurls to read freedom. (0.6s)
