# O60 The Last Emperor

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Blockbuster Era 1980–1989
- Looks like: Iron-wire-line drawing on silk with layer upon layer of azurite, malachite, cinnabar and gold paint, ruled-line architectural palaces, thousands of kneeling court officials. The boy emperor's enthronement is a Qing court ceremony, so it is painted the way Qing court painters recorded ceremonies.
- Style: gongbi heavy-color painting · court painting on silk
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1987

## Style prompt


---

English:

Chinese gongbi zhongcai (fine-line, heavy-colour court painting) on aged silk. Far to near, each form: flat base, a wash darkening its lower, overlapped edges, an even iron-wire outline (dark brown, ~1/900 frame width), gold and white lifts. Last, a 30% warm ochre glaze over everything, lines still clear. Minerals: cinnabar #C23A22, azurite #2C5A9A, malachite #3A8A6A, gamboge #E8B830, gold #CAA04A, muted by the glaze; ground and sky mid-value warm silk ochre, never near-white, saturated blue or orange; time of day shows only as overall warmth. No cast shadows or glare. Fine-line water ripples. Ruled-line architecture, every tile drawn; crowds all differ; a lone figure works. Faint silk weave (±3%), brown vignette. Symmetrical one-point composition: a large figure before a deep, ordered space. Slow pushes. Reference: Qing court scrolls, e.g. *Kangxi's Southern Inspection Tour*. Avoid: copy-pasted figures; unwashed flat fills; loose ink wash; poster saturation.

### Director's notes

Clawd plays the child Puyi: a court hat with a red tassel, court beads, horse-hoof sleeves. The curtains part and Clawd steps out on the beat; a hard cut to the wave of officials kowtowing, with the last few beats landing on a poster-like wide shot. The scene plays at 1.1x speed.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. A yellow silk curtain embroidered with roundel dragons fills the picture; the curtain parts and Clawd, in a court hat with a red tassel, walks up onto the imperial terrace; the camera pulls back slightly. (1s)
2. Hard cut to a low-position lateral move sweeping across the kneeling officials, row upon row in rank-badge robes kowtowing in waves. (0.6s)
3. Hard cut to a poster composition that slowly widens: Clawd stands large on the imperial terrace, behind him a square full of kneeling officials and golden double-eaved roofs, a cricket jar in the foreground. (0.9s)
