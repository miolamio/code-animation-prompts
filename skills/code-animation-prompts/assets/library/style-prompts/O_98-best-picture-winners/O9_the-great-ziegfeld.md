English:

Real 3D stage spectacle in 1930s studio silver: the subject's world built as a revue set on a round tiered platform turning about 3° per second, draped and garlanded; its people become rows of costumed chorus figures with faces and arms. Costumes light satin grey, set mid-grey, pleated curtain backdrop darker, theatre deep black; whites below pure white. Soft front key; brighter rim lights edging satin and feathers near-white. Faint shafts, grain (±3%). All neutral grey except the hero the story centres on (e.g. #D97757); lamps render white. Signature shot: low medium along the tiers, figures 1/4-1/3 of frame height, the hero on the front tier. Then a 3 s curtain rise, a 10 s crane up to a level medium of the hero. Reference: Robert Z. Leonard, *The Great Ziegfeld* (1936). Avoid: flat lighting, faceless cones, fast spinning.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

three.js 3D, MGM silver black-and-white, 1936 musical spectacle, ostrich feathers and chiffon

### Director's notes

Clawd is the star atop the cake: a rhinestone headband with a plume of ostrich feathers cascading like a fountain. The scene runs at 1.2x; the star's face is held back until the last shot, when on beat 5 it turns, hops, and throws both arms up into a V. Two hard cuts link the curtain, the crane and the top tier.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Front-on low camera position: a chiffon curtain flies up from the rotating cake stage, revealing tier after tier of women holding fans, with a tiny Clawd at the top; the camera tilts upward. (0.9s)
2. Rising along the spiral crane move, sweeping past ostrich-feather fans, a white grand piano and violinists, and stopping just below the top tier. (0.8s)
3. A near eye-level medium shot: Clawd at the top, wearing an ostrich-feather headdress, turns to face the camera and throws both arms into a V; slow push-in. (0.8s)
