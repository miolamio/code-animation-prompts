## Style prompt

Bob Fosse-style Broadway stage number. Black stage, but about 40% of the frame glows signal red #D0202A: a row of dark barred cells, each backlit red through haze, bright at centre, falling off to black, plus red curtains; hard-edged spots. Dancers are full-bodied black silhouettes with costume shapes and a 2–3 px red rim. One big neon shape (letter or pictogram) in orange-red #FF5A3C, white-hot core, slight flicker, set against black so it reads; glossy black floor reflects it; marquee bulbs #FFD9A0 at the stage edge. The hero, in a warm-white follow spot #FFF1D6, is the only lit figure in its own colours. Accents: sequins, white gloves. Moves on the jazz beat; cells snap on one by one. Push in to end in the follow spot. Reference: Rob Marshall, *Chicago* (2002). Avoid: flat even fills; extra colours; rimless silhouettes.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Bob Fosse's stage direction, red-rimmed backlit silhouettes, neon and sequins, the red and black of the 2002 poster

### Director's notes

Clawd wears a Fosse-style bowler hat (the hat band ringed with sequins), white gloves, and carries a black cane. In the first shot the cells light up one by one on the beat; quick cut to a single prisoner; then Clawd takes the follow spot alone and finishes with jazz hands. The performance runs at real speed, no speed-up.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Wide shot of the stage with the neon C behind: the six cells light up one at a time, each prisoner snapping into her signature pose, Clawd doing a Fosse walk at center stage; slow push-in. (0.8s)
2. Close shot: a single cell, Velma's silhouette in red light behind the bars, hips swiveling, shoulders shrugging; slight push-in. No Clawd. (0.5s)
3. Medium close shot: Clawd alone in the white follow spot with the lit cells behind: he does the Fosse walk up to the footlights, then lowers the cane and raises both hands in jazz hands; slow push-in. (1.2s)
