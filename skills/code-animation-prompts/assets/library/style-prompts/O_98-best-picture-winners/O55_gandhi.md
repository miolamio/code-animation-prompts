# O55 Gandhi

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Blockbuster Era 1980–1989

## Style prompt


---

English:

Hand block print on cotton, Rajasthan style (Bagru, Sanganer). Undyed cotton #EEE5CF is the ground, over half the frame, and the skin colour; indigo #2A3A68 for water or sky bands and patterns; madder red #AA322A for borders, clothing and the hero; turmeric #DEA42C for small motifs; black key block #281E1C for outlines. Stamp blocks about 1/8 of frame height, misregistered 2–4 px, tone jumping slightly at seams even in large bands; dye bleeds along the weave, fills unevenly. Mud-resist crackle only inside indigo, fine and sparse. Carved, rough outlines about 1/300 of frame width. Flat frieze in a paisley and vine border; waves as scallop rows; profile figures with miniature-painting faces, kohl-lined eyes. Camera: pan along the frieze, push in; cut on a turning motif. Avoid: vector edges, perfect registration, faceless figures, dense crackle, indigo flooding the frame.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Bagru mud-resist indigo, Sanganer wood-block printed borders, Rajasthani miniature-painting profile faces

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Gandhi: round wire-rimmed glasses, the edge of a khadi shawl, a bamboo staff, a handful of salt. 1.1x speed throughout, no slow motion. Hard cuts from the procession to the action: bending down, scooping up the salt, raising it, then turning to the marchers and raising it higher. It ends with a transition through a turning spinning-wheel.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Pan along the procession on the coast: volunteers in white khadi, women in indigo and madder-red saris, the swaraj flag; Clawd enters at the head of the line. (0.8s)
2. Medium close shot, slow push-in: Clawd in round glasses and a khadi shawl bends down and scoops up a handful of salt, lifts it so the grains glitter, then turns to the marchers and raises it higher. (1.1s)
3. A village woman sits spinning thread at a spinning wheel; the camera pushes toward the turning wheel, leading into the next film. (0.6s)
