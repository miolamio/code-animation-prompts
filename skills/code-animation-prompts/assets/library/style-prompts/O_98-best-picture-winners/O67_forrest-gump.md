## Style prompt


---

English:

A real pop-up book filmed on a desk. Every element: a flat die-cut card on parallel folds before a 90° backdrop page, printed with transparent watercolour and grainy coloured-pencil hatching; cut edges show a white core and grey board. Default light: bright window daylight from the upper left, soft shadows between layers. Print colours (sky blue, warm sand, greens) and room light stay daylight whatever the story's time of day; that time appears only in the print (e.g. a low sun, a night sky). Walnut desk #6B3E1E; the room a warm blur. Physical depth of field, focus on the hero's layer. Signature shot: a high three-quarter view of the whole spread. One continuous move: dive into a page, pull back, turn the page instead of cutting, push into the next spread. References: Robert Sabuda, Matthew Reinhart. Avoid: solid-looking cards; dark moody rooms; cartoon faces.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Robert Sabuda pop-up book structure, watercolor and colored-pencil print texture, three.js paper cards, physical depth of field

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Forrest: holding a box of chocolates on the bench, wearing a red baseball cap on the road and on the boat. One shot of 12 beats with no cut: a feather carries the camera into the book, and two page turns (at about 2.7 seconds and 4.2 seconds) replace the editing. He starts running at 3.25 seconds, then stops and turns around right after.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Single continuous take: the closed book on the desk opens and the square in Savannah stands up; the camera follows a falling white feather down to the bench, where Clawd hands a chocolate to the nurse; pull back, turn the page, and push in to US-163 in Monument Valley, where Clawd in a red cap leads a crowd of scruffy followers as they run closer, stop, and he turns to face them; pull back and turn the page again, then push in to the shrimp boat "Jenny" in a storm full of lightning. (5s)
