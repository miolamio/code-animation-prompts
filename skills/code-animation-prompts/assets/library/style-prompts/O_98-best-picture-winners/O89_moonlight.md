# O89 Moonlight

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Indie Era 2010–2019
- Looks like: Oil portraits in saturated teal, magenta and moonlight blue, sculpted heads repainted with brushstrokes that follow the form. Painted straight from the line in the film's title: "In moonlight, black boys look blue."
- Style: moonlight-blue oil portrait
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 2016

## Style prompt


---

English:

Night oil portraiture: smooth volumes repainted in two passes of short, low-contrast (±8% value) form-following strokes: broad (1/60 of frame width) on water, sky and cloth, fine (1/150) on faces, which stay smooth. Palette by role: sky, sea and far land mid blue-violet #32377a, about 60% of the frame; moonlit planes and sand pale lavender-blue; teal #128a91 in water and lit sides; magenta #7a2f86 rim; deepest shadows #07071c. Any skin takes blue top planes and a wet highlight, eyes open and lit. Default night: one key, a full moon with a broken glitter path on water; magenta only as a soft rim from a distant glow; other small light sources stay cyan-white points. Grain ±3%. Signature shot: the figure calm, ~2/3 of frame height. Slow push-ins. Reference: Barry Jenkins, *Moonlight* (2016). Avoid: coarse dabs, small dark statue eyes, a murky frame.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Oil portrait brushwork, teal / magenta / blue-violet duotone, half-above, half-below-water camera position

### Director's notes

Clawd plays the child Chiron, in no costume: first held up on the water in a swimming lesson, then standing among three faces, finally sitting on the beach beside Kevin. Three chapters, three hard-cut shots; the shots in the sea and on the beach both push in slowly to carry the quiet on.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Half-above, half-below-water shot hugging the emerald sea surface: a man stands chest-deep in the water cradling Clawd in his palm, his hand visible underwater; slow push-in. (0.8s)
2. Cut to three oil portraits side by side, one teal, one magenta, one blue-violet; the camera reframes and the three faces slide together into a wedge-shaped collage, with Clawd standing in the teal piece. (0.8s)
3. Cut to the beach in moonlight: a two-shot of Kevin and Clawd sitting side by side on blue sand, a full moon at upper right, surf breaking on the shore; slow push-in. (0.9s)
