# O78 Crash

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · New Millennium 2000–2009
- Looks like: A retouched night photograph drawn in code: white and red headlight trails on an overpass, sodium-lamp orange against neon cyan, reflections on a wet road, out-of-focus bokeh, and snow that is only visible in the light. The ending of Crash is a rare snowfall in Los Angeles.

- Keywords: 2D

## Style prompt


---

English:

Long-exposure night street photography. Sodium-vapour orange #F2A24A washes structures, signs and wet ground (about half the frame in warm light), against cyan neon #3FD0D8 and the odd red-blue emergency light; blacks cyan-tinged, never grey. Layered composition: foreground pavement with figures, lit shopfronts and signs mid-frame, roads or bridges crossing the upper third (away from cities, a lamp-lit road in the subject's own setting). Headlights become thin continuous ribbons (about 1/150 of frame width) along those roads, white #FFF4E0 and red #E0303A, glowing with hot cores. Snow: three layers of short soft streaks, seen only in light cones. Finish: two-level bloom, blue lens streaks, edge bokeh, vignette, moving grain. The hero stands sharp in a lamp cone mid-frame; only traffic streaks. Camera: one continuous tilt, pan and rack focus. Reference: cinematographer J. Michael Muro (*Crash*, 2004). Avoid: evenly lit snow; empty black frames; dotted trails.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Long-exposure night photography, sodium orange with neon cyan, anamorphic widescreen light streaks and film grain

### Director's notes

Clawd wears a hand-knit dark teal scarf and stands under a streetlamp, looking up at the snow. One continuous camera move goes from the light trails to the crash to Clawd, with the fast-moving sections carrying motion blur. As the camera reaches him the snow gets bigger, and the snow-catching gesture lands near the end.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Single continuous take: it opens with a low-angle shot of light trails on an interchange, tilts down to a rear-end collision behind the caution tape with focus on the accident; then pans right along the curb to Clawd under a sodium lamp as focus pulls over; Clawd raises an arm to catch a snowflake and blinks. (2.5s)
