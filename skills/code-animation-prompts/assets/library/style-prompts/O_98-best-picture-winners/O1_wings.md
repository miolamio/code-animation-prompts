# O1 Wings

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Silent and Early Sound 1927–1938
- Looks like: a WWI dogfight built in three.js and graded like a 1927 orthochromatic print: whitened sky, very deep blacks, an overall amber tint, gate weave and scratches. *Wings* is famous for aerial combat shot with cameras strapped to the planes, so most camera positions here are mounted on aircraft.
- Style: silent-era 3D air combat · orthochromatic black-and-white + amber tint
- Keywords: 3D·B&W
- Year: 1927/28

## Style prompt

Smooth-shaded 3D graded as a silent-era orthochromatic print: high contrast, reds go black. Sky near-white, ground in readable mid-greys, deep blacks (25-35% of frame) only in silhouettes. Tint: blend whites half-way toward amber #FFCC80, so the sky is pale cream-amber, never solid orange; blacks stay black. Only the hero the story centres on keeps colour, whole figure at ~70% saturation (e.g. #D97757); every lamp, fire or other light source stays grey. 24 fps gate weave (±1 px), flicker (±5%), fine grain (±4%), 1-3 thin scratches, sparse dust, rounded corners, vignette; open on a 1 s iris. Signature shot: a camera rigged to something moving with the subject, rig in frame; then chase and POV. Cut every 2-3 s; white out through cloud, smoke or glare. Reference: William Wellman, *Wings* (1927). Avoid: motion blur, colour in the greys.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

three.js 3D, orthochromatic black-and-white, silent-era tinting, projection wear and iris-in

### Director's notes

Clawd plays a US Army pilot: leather flying helmet, goggles, white scarf, seated in a SPAD cockpit. The opening prologue leads into the first shot, and the iris opens on beat 1; then five hard cuts, roughly one cut every two beats. Around beat 5 he looks back and the machine guns fire; the last shot passes through cloud into a white-out that hands off to the cream white at the start of Part 2.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Straight on at the nose, facing the cockpit: Clawd in a leather flying helmet, goggles pushed up on his forehead, framed between two struts; the iris opens and the camera pushes in slowly. (0.9s)
2. Big wide shot: a squadron circles among the clouds over checkerboard fields and chalk trenches below, one Fokker trailing black smoke; the camera pushes in slowly. (0.6s)
3. Nose-mounted camera with engine vibration: Clawd looks back at the Fokker on his tail and raises one hand, the twin Vickers machine guns flashing as they fire. (0.8s)
4. Looking out from the Fokker's upper wing, the enemy machine guns fire, chasing Clawd's SPAD ahead; the camera is fixed to the enemy plane. (0.8s)
5. A diving shot over Clawd's shoulder: the upper wing and bracing wires in front, shell craters and zigzag trenches rushing up to meet the camera. (1.1s)
6. The SPAD in a bank of cloud flies toward the camera like a ghost, the fog thickening until the picture whites out to cream. (0.8s)
