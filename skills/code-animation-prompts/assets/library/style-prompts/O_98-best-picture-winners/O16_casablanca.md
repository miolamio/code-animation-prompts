## Style prompt


---

English:

1940s Hollywood studio black-and-white, painted like a retouched glamour still. Forms: smooth airbrushed tone blocks. Faces: short key (light on the face's far side from camera; the near cheek in shadow, holding a Rembrandt triangle of light), two catchlights per eye. Slatted shadows from anything at hand (blinds, railings, branches) fall diagonally across surfaces; light shafts show only in smoke or fog. Values: 30-40% of the frame deep black, whites small and glowing; outdoor skies mid-dark. Grade: diffusion glow, an S-curve, fine grain (±3%), soft vignette, faint flicker; fog stays local. Only the hero the story centres on keeps colour, on its body or costume (e.g. #D97757); skin and lamps stay grey. Camera: a long glide, a push to a face, a pull-back into fog. References: cinematographer Arthur Edeson (*Casablanca*, Michael Curtiz, 1942). Avoid: flat lighting, grey haze, stiff busts.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

airbrushed retouched-still portraits, Arthur Edeson studio lighting, diffusion filter and silver-halide grain

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Rick: bow tie and a champagne coupe in the cafe, a fedora and a turned-up trench coat collar at the airport. The cafe is one unbroken camera move that ends on him raising his glass ("Here's looking at you, kid"); the exact midpoint beat is the only hard cut in the piece, jumping to the airport, then a look back and one long pull back to finish.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. One camera move through Rick's cafe: gliding past the ceiling fan and smoke to Sam at the piano and Ilsa leaning on it, pushing in on her soft-focus face as tears begin to well, then moving laterally across the whole room to Clawd in a bow tie at a table, raising his champagne glass. (2.5s)
2. Hard cut to the fog-bound airport: a close shot of Clawd in a fedora and turned-up trench coat collar, glancing back; the camera pulls back a long way to reveal Ilsa, Captain Renault and the Electra airliner with propellers turning in the fog. (2.5s)
