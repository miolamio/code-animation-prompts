## Style prompt

Ben Shahn's social-realist tempera. Ink lines (about 1/500 of frame width) jitter and wander, break each outline into two or three strokes, sometimes doubled by a fainter offset ghost (a blotted line). Flat tempera blocks sit 2-4 px off the line, rough-edged, dry-brush streaks (one brush wide, about 1/100 of frame width) over a third of each block; model form with a darker flat of the same hue. Palette by role: slate #768492 sky and cool areas, brick #9E4834 and ochre #C89852 ground, buildings, clothes, ink #1F1A1A, warm board #F0E9DA; skin pale ochre; other colours: these darkened or greyed. The hero the story centres on wears the one warm accent on its main garment (e.g. #D97757). Big heads and hands, long faces, heavy jaws, heavy clothes. Camera: short pushes, tilt up, close-up. Avoid: smooth gradients, clean closed outlines, cheerful colour. Shadow flats are hard-edged. Clothes hang and sag.

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Terry: a dark navy knit cap, a red-and-black checked collar and cuffs, a pigeon perched on its hand. A hard cut from grabbing hands on the dock to a quiet rooftop sets this film's two worlds side by side in two shots; the middle shot has the flock of pigeons rising, and the close shot rests on Clawd and the pigeon. Performance at 1.8x speed.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Medium shot of the morning shape-up at the PIER B gate: dockworkers crowd the entrance, hands after hands reaching up to grab brass tags, Clawd not in the frame; gentle push-in. (0.8s)
2. Wide shot of the rooftop pigeon coop: Clawd in a knit cap and standing checked collar holds up a hand with a pigeon on it, Edie beside him, a water tower and the Manhattan skyline behind and a flock of pigeons rising; slow push-in with a slight tilt up. (1s)
3. Close shot of Clawd and the gray pigeon on its hand, a freighter, rooftops and the skyline behind its shoulder; gentle push-in. (0.8s)
