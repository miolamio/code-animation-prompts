# O53 Ordinary People

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Blockbuster Era 1980–1989
- Looks like: Layered transparent watercolor: wet-in-wet sky and lake, edge lines left by water pooling, backruns, bare paper as highlights, autumn gold and rust red set against cool grey-blue. It suits the quiet, slightly sad Lake Forest autumn that opens the film.
- Style: autumn transparent watercolor
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1980

## Style prompt

Transparent watercolour in layered glazes on cold-press paper. Draw shapes crisply first, then two or three glazes, each smaller and darker, drying to a tide-mark edge; two or three blooms, about 1/10 of frame width, inside large washes, never on figures; light granulation (±4%). Paper is the brightest white; light objects and highlights left as paper. Sky and water wet-in-wet, ripples dry-brush. Roles: autumn gold #ECAA34, scarlet #E46030, rust #B8743C for foliage and accents, against grey-blue mist #C2C6D2 and violet-grey shadows #8A8496; sky pale and cool at any time of day; ground pale green-ochre. Foliage: pale wash, scallop-edged clusters, dark dots, flicked leaves; with few trees, warm colours go to scrub, roofs, clothes. Light from one side. Camera: slow pan, gentle push, leaves drifting past the lens. Reference: Winslow Homer's watercolours. Avoid: opaque gouache, airbrushed blur, vector edges, muddy colour, saturated sunsets.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Layered transparent watercolor, Winslow Homer and John Singer Sargent, HB pencil underdrawing

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Conrad: a long navy-and-cream school scarf lifted by the lake wind, a leather satchel on his back. 1.1x speed throughout, as slow as the opening of Pachelbel's Canon. Three hard cuts: the lake, the son looking back at the house, the unresponsive mother. His gaze moving from the house to the ground is the entire performance.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Scenic shot of Lake Michigan at dawn: a path of sunlight left as bare paper on the grey-blue water; the camera slowly pans right to the autumn bluff. (0.8s)
2. A street under maple trees: Clawd as Conrad, in a long striped scarf and with a satchel on his back, stops on the sidewalk and looks back at the white Colonial house (Calvin raking leaves in the yard), then lowers his eyes and turns away; the camera slowly moves right and pulls back slightly. (1s)
3. Beth on the porch with her arms folded, her expression cold; the camera slowly pushes in on her. (0.7s)
