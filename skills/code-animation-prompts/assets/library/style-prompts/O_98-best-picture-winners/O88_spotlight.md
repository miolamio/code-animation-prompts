# O88 Spotlight

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Indie Era 2010–2019
- Looks like: A picture printed in newspaper halftone dots, placed on the grey-green screen of a microfilm reader: scanlines, a rolling scan band, dust on the glass. The reporters in the film dig up the truth in exactly this kind of archive, and the cuts are made as microfilm lateral slides.
- Style: microfilm reader · newspaper halftone
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 2015

## Style prompt


---

English:

A 2001 newspaper colour halftone on a library microfiche reader. Signature shot: the picture fills the frame, no headline or columns; lines and lettering inside it stay solid. CMYK screens at 15°, 75°, 0°, 45°, dots about 1/250 of frame width, 1 px misregistration. Convert to low-contrast phosphor-green monochrome, dots still clearly visible: film base #1b2320, frame gaps #0c110f, highlights no brighter than #a0dcaf. Only the story's one hero keeps its colour, whole figure (e.g. #D97757), screened and equally low-contrast; every light source turns green. Scan lines every 3 px, a faint scan bar every 4 s, a lamp hot-spot, dust, a hair, a light rounded hood vignette (outer ~5%), a small monospace fiche index. Scene changes slide the fiche sideways past a dark gap (0.5 s); slow push-ins. Avoid: newspaper layout, colour leaking, hard cuts.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Library microfilm reader, 2001 newspaper CMYK halftone dots, phosphor-green monochrome

### Director's notes

Clawd plays a reporter on the Spotlight team: a lanyard ID badge, a reporter's notebook, later swapped for a highlighter. Three microfilm frames, joined not by hard cuts but by the film sliding sideways inside the reader, with the last beat landing on the front page.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Wide shot of the basement newsroom: reporters around a desk piled with paper, a fluorescent light grid overhead, Clawd in front with an ID badge and a notebook; the camera slowly pulls back. (0.8s)
2. The film slides sideways to the next frame: a high-angle view of the printed list of priests, a toy-sized Clawd pushing a huge highlighter down the list line by line, the camera moving down with it. (0.8s)
3. Slides again to the printing plant dock: medium close shot, Clawd standing beside the front page, the headline reading "Church allowed abuse by priest for years"; slow push-in. (0.9s)
