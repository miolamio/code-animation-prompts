## Style prompt

1959 65mm widescreen epic built in 3D. Default: hard high sun (about 50°), short crisp shadows, cobalt sky fading to sandy white; under a low sun shadows stay hard but long, the sky turns amber. Marble, gilt, red granite and sandstone, with mouldings and fluted columns, never bare boxes. Warm sunlit haze mixes about 20% into mid-distance colours. Technicolor-print grade: warm, saturated, slightly contrasty, no clipping or bloom; fine grain (±3%), slight gate weave. Scale is the point: monumental architecture, statues and repeated elements fill the background, never empty. The one hero subject (e.g. orange #D97757) is the most saturated thing in frame; any repeated motion (stride, wheel, oar) keeps time with speed. Signature shot: low tracking beside the moving hero, 35–50 mm full-frame; then wide, top-down, close-up, low angle. Avoid: clean CG light, empty or primitive sets, overexposure. The haze is warm dust.

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Judah Ben-Hur: a linen headband across his forehead, reins in one hand and a whip raised in the other, driving four white horses. Six shots in five seconds, run through the way the 1959 second unit shot it: the start, the turn, tracking, the wheels, the close shot, the poster composition. Every camera position moves with the chariot, and the rhythm never drops.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Big wide shot of the starting line: nine four-horse chariots side by side, with Clawd's white team among them and the grandstands packed behind; the field bursts out and the camera tightens slowly. (0.9s)
2. High-angle shot at the east turn: the chariots swing wide around the gilded turning posts, throwing up arcs of dust behind them. (0.8s)
3. Parallel tracking shot beside Clawd's chariot: the white horses at full gallop, spokes whirling, dust flying; the camera moves forward with the chariot. (1.1s)
4. Low camera position between the two chariots: Messala's serrated hub blades bite into Clawd's wheel, sparks flying; the camera travels along with the chariots. (0.6s)
5. Close shot of Clawd cracking the whip from the chariot, Messala's black horses beside him; slow push-in. (0.8s)
6. Poster composition: a low-angle side shot with Clawd's white team drawing level beneath the golden statue of the colossus on the central barrier, the grandstands and the governor's box behind; the camera widens slowly. (0.8s)
