# O24 An American in Paris

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Widescreen 1950–1959
- Looks like: Raoul Dufy's watercolor: quick blue-black ink lines over transparent patches of cobalt blue, lemon yellow and rose pink, the line and the color never lining up. The film's closing ballet set was designed after Dufy's paintings, and the Place de la Concorde and the dancers are drawn his way.
- Style: Dufy watercolor ballet
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1951

## Style prompt


---

English:

Paint in Raoul Dufy's watercolour manner: colour first, line second, never aligned. Transparent washes (wet edges, pooled rims, granulation) cover most of the frame, 10-40% oversized, slid one way by about 1/30 of frame width: cobalt #2258CE for sky and water, lemon #FCE02E for light and ground, rose #EC3A7C for warm planes. Overlaps mix violet or green, never brown; keep the hero's wash off blue. Then draw the whole scene densely in quick, thin blue-black ink (about 1/700 of frame width) with flicked ends: buildings, windows, figures, extras; straight edges overshoot corners; trees and clouds as looped strings. Ink #1E1E3A; bare paper 20-30%, skin included. The hero the story centres on gets its own wash (e.g. #D97757). Figures bounce. Camera: small pushes, cuts on the beat (or every 2 s). Avoid: muddy colour, lines tracing the colour, sparse line.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

loose ink line + offset color patches, Raoul Dufy, Minnelli's ballet sets, cold-pressed watercolor paper

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Jerry from the poster: a cobalt-blue beret tilted back (Gene Kelly's), a small rose-pink neckerchief, a brush flinging a ribbon of paint. In all three shots it is dancing on every beat, and the dance steps themselves are the editing rhythm: the square, a close shot with Lise, a street full of flags. Performance at 1.8x speed.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Wide shot of the Place de la Concorde: dancers whirl around the fountain with the obelisk and the Eiffel Tower behind them; Clawd as Jerry, in a cobalt hat and holding a brush, dances beside Lise in her yellow dress; the camera pushes in slightly. (1s)
2. Close shot of Clawd in mid-air: the brush flings a ribbon of paint and Lise's arm reaches into the frame; slow push-in. (0.8s)
3. Cut to a Paris street strung with tricolor flags, the buildings pink, yellow and blue, Clawd bounding among the pedestrians in the middle of the street; slow push-in. (0.8s)
