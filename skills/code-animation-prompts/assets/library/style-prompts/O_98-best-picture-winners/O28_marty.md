English:

A 1950s kinescope playing on a wood-cabinet television. Inside the screen: black-and-white with a cool phosphor tint (#DCE6EA whites), soft (half resolution, scaled up), halation, ghosting, tearing bands, a rolling hum bar, light snow, lifted blacks, flicker, grain; only the story's hero keeps its colour (e.g. #D97757), under the same artefacts. Tube: rounded corners, sides bowing outward, about 330 scanlines, glass reflections. Outside, a warm flat-illustrated living room (ochre wallpaper, wood, a table lamp), lit warm by the lamp; the screen casts a clear cold-blue glow on nearby walls. Signature shot: hold about 2 s with the TV filling 60% of the frame, then push through the glass (4-5 s) until the broadcast fills the frame, scanlines visible, then steady TV-studio mid shots. Reference: director Delbert Mann's live Philco Television Playhouse dramas. Avoid: heavy snow, a dull, evenly lit room.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

kinescope black-and-white recording, rounded picture tube and scanlines, warm 1950s living-room illustration

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Marty: a white butcher's paper hat and a striped tie, leaning shyly against a pillar. The push into the glass carries us from the living room into the program; in the two-shot it summons its courage to cross the dance floor, and the last hard cut lands on the invitation to dance: it holds out a hand, and Clara's smile only comes afterward.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Mother's living room: a lamp and a rotary telephone at left, her wingback chair at right, the television showing the Stardust Ballroom; the camera pushes into the screen glass. (0.8s)
2. A two-shot across the dance floor, scanlines still visible: Clawd in a butcher's paper hat and striped tie crosses from the pillar toward Clara; the camera tracks right. (0.9s)
3. Medium shot of the invitation to dance: Clawd holds out a hand, Clara looks up at it and smiles; gentle push-in and a drift to the right. (0.8s)
