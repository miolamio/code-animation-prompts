# O81 Slumdog Millionaire

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · New Millennium 2000–2009
- Looks like: A blue-violet TV studio with gold highlights, moving-head light beams and lens flares; then a cut to saturated orange-and-teal Mumbai rooftops. The film itself cuts back and forth between the final question of the quiz show and the slum where Jamal grew up.
- Style: quiz show studio + Mumbai orange and teal
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 2008

## Style prompt

Two registers. Primary, about 70%: a glossy illustrated quiz-show studio, bright and full, seen three-quarter from slightly above: a glass stage lit from below with concentric blue rings, a packed audience behind, moving-head beams cutting blue-violet #5A3FD0 through haze, a big screen of elongated-hexagon answers, one locked in amber #F0A020; bloom and horizontal flares; as tension rises the edges dim to about half, never black. Characters rounded, softly shaded. Second register, about 30%, as full-frame hard cuts: golden-hour views of the subject's home ground, saturated orange and teal, busy (e.g. tin roofs, washing lines, crowds). Accents: magenta #E2206B, teal #0F7C7A, tangerine #F06A14. Camera: push in on screen and hot seat as beams close on one person; elsewhere slow pans. Reference: cinematographer Anthony Dod Mantle (*Slumdog Millionaire*, 2008). Avoid: real show logos; a mostly black studio; even lighting; grey, empty places.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

TV quiz show lighting, blue-violet beams and lens flares, Anthony Dod Mantle's orange-and-teal palette

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Jamal: a light blue fine-check shirt collar with a clip-on mic. The answer is locked in from the first frame; the second shot dims the whole studio and narrows to the hot seat, releasing when the answer turns green; then a hard cut to the Mumbai rooftops opens the picture out to the whole city at once.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Close shot: the final question on the big screen, four answer boxes, A: ARAMIS locking in to amber; slight push-in. No Clawd. (0.8s)
2. Wide shot of the stage: the host sits on a stool, Clawd as Jamal in the hot seat; the studio goes dark and the beams converge on Clawd, then the answer flashes green and the audience cheers; slow push-in. (1s)
3. Wide shot: golden-hour Dharavi rooftops, Clawd standing on a tin roof as a train packed with people passes on the elevated line behind him; the camera slowly pans right. (0.8s)
