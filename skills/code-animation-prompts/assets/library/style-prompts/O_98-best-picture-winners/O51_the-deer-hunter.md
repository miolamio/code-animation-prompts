# O51 The Deer Hunter

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · New Hollywood 1970–1979
- Looks like: Egg tempera in the manner of Andrew Wyeth: dry, fine cross-hatched short strokes in low-saturation straw yellow, ochre brown and grey green, every blade of grass drawn individually, a pale sky. Wyeth was a Pennsylvania painter, and the one-shot deer hunt takes place in the mountains above a Pennsylvania steel town.
- Style: Andrew Wyeth egg tempera
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1978

## Style prompt


---

English:

Egg tempera on gesso panel after Andrew Wyeth. Dry, matte, very fine: colour built from dense cross-hatched strokes, each about 1/400 of frame width long, hairline thin. Muted palette: pale overcast sky #D9D5C6, straw grass #CDB27A, umber #6A5234, misty grey-green hills #8E938C, rust #8A4E32; water, stone, clothes and skin mixed from these. One accent colour, only on the one hero subject (e.g. a red cap); any light source is a small pale dot, no halo. Ground (dry grass by default, other ground alike): big light and dark patches, fine strokes, dark tufts, then single wind-combed blades; rocks, buildings hatched along their planes. Empty, lonely space; the subject small, off-centre. Soft overcast light; at other times of day stay pale, no warm glow. Camera: slow drift, quiet push; a circular iris may mark a cut. Avoid: wet watercolour blooms, saturated colour, thick shiny strokes.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Gesso-panel egg tempera, Andrew Wyeth, fine cross-hatched short strokes

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Mike: a red wool hunting cap and a rifle with a scope. Valley, then scope, then deer eye, then hunter: four hard cuts at an average of about 1.24x speed, quiet and steady. The performance beat is the shot that is never fired: the rifle comes down, eyes close, a breath out, then a look up at the deer.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Wide shot of the steel town in the valley mist: blast furnaces, smoking chimneys, an onion-domed church; slow lateral camera move. (0.6s)
2. Scope point of view: a stag on the ridge inside a black circular mask and crosshairs; the picture sways slightly with the hunter's hand. (0.7s)
3. Close-up of the stag's head and eye; slow push-in. (0.3s)
4. Clawd as Mike stands on granite, lowers the rifle, closes his eyes and breathes out, then looks up at the stag; the camera pulls back to take in the whole ridge. (0.8s)
