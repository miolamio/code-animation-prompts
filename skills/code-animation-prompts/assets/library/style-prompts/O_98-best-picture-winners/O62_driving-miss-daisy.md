## Style prompt

Norman Rockwell-style mid-century magazine-cover oil illustration. Near-realistic figures: head about 1/7 of body height, faces and hands modelled from 3–4 value planes, not outlines; readable expressions. Smooth modelling; faint strokes about 1/150 of frame width, following form, each within ±5% of the colour beneath. Default light: warm afternoon sun from the upper left, clear cast and dappled shadows in cool blue-grey; other light keeps a warm key and cool shadows. Palette: cream sky #E6DCC2, brick red #9A4A38, leaf greens, bottle green #1F3A24, soft skin. Gloss reflects surroundings. Background storytelling: busy bystanders where there are people, telling objects when the hero is alone. Light canvas texture, warm varnish, soft vignette. Camera: deep three-quarter wide, slow push, closer cuts ending on a hand or face. Reference: Rockwell's *Saturday Evening Post* covers (1940s–50s). Avoid: cartoon faces; texture filters; flat digital shading; empty backgrounds. 1950s American magazine cover. Medium saturation.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Norman Rockwell oil illustration, Saturday Evening Post covers, 3D-modeled car overpainted with oil brushwork

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Hoke, in a navy driver's cap. Three hard cuts from outside the car to inside the car to the rearview mirror, each closer than the last; the payoff is saved for the end, as Miss Daisy's stern face in the mirror slowly softens into a smile over the last few beats. Scene time runs about 1.8x screen time, and the car still drives at an unhurried pace.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Front three-quarter view of a dark green 1949 Hudson, driving slowly down a street lined with oak trees, red brick houses and white porches; Clawd in a driver's cap holds the wheel, and Miss Daisy sits stern-faced in the back window; slow push-in. (0.9s)
2. Hard cut to a locked-off shot from the back seat looking forward: a large side profile of Miss Daisy in the foreground, the back of Clawd driving behind her, the street scene flowing past the car window. (0.8s)
3. Hard cut to a close-up of the rearview mirror, the poster composition: on the left Clawd's eyes under the cap brim, on the right Miss Daisy slowly letting a small smile show; gentle push-in. (0.8s)
