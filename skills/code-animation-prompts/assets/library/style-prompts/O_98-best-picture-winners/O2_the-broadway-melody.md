# O2 The Broadway Melody

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Silent and Early Sound 1927–1938
- Looks like: a moving black, gold and silver Art Deco musical poster: airbrush gradients, a stepped proscenium, chrome condenser microphones. Broadway in 1929 was at the peak of Art Deco, and this was the film that brought sound pictures, so sound waves ring outward from the microphone.
- Style: black, gold and silver Art Deco musical poster
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1928/29

## Style prompt

Art Deco revue poster. Black lacquer ground; gold and silver from banded metallic gradients (3-5 hard bands); airbrush glows, sunbursts, ziggurat steps, streamlined curves; a stepped proscenium frame of gold lines and bulbs. Palette: ground #0A0908, gold #D6B560, highlight #F3E2A2, silver #BFC4C9; skin pale silver-cream; one saturated accent (e.g. #D97757) only on the hero the story centres on. Elongated Erté-style figures with real faces; fringe, sequins or feathers where the subject allows. Fine grain, glints, stars. Frontal symmetry, a central spotlight. Motion: chasing bulbs, a row of repeated elements (dancers, waves, columns) rippling toward the centre, rings radiating from the main sound or light source; slow pushes, cut on the music's beat (without music, every 2-3 s). References: A. M. Cassandre's posters, Erté's costumes. Avoid: flat yellow for metal, faceless figures, extra hues.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Art Deco poster, airbrushed metallic gradients, A. M. Cassandre, Erté costumes

### Director's notes

Clawd is a tap dancer in a stiff straw boater with a black cane, tapping from start to finish. It fades in from the white of Part 1; on beat 4 the microphone comes alive and sound waves pour out (the talkies have arrived). Four hard cuts: street corner → stage → microphone → chorus line → Clawd.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Fade in from white: the marquee of the Astor Theatre (THE BROADWAY MELODY / ALL TALKING · ALL SINGING · ALL DANCING) with chasing bulbs; Clawd stands at left in a stiff straw boater with a cane; the camera pulls back slowly. (0.8s)
2. Wide shot of the stage: an Art Deco proscenium, chorus girls on the steps, Clawd tap-dancing beside a giant condenser microphone that begins to ring out sound waves; the camera pulls back slowly. (1s)
3. Close-up of the chrome spring-mounted ring microphone, sound waves radiating and musical notes flying out; the camera pushes in. (0.5s)
4. A lateral move along the kick line: feathered headdresses and sequined short skirts, rows of legs kicking in a wave. (0.5s)
5. Close shot of Clawd, his straw boater tilting from side to side as he taps, the microphone ring behind him; a gentle push-in. (0.5s)
