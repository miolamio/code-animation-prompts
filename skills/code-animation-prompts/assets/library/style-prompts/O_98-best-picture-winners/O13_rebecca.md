# O13 Rebecca

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Color and War 1939–1949
- Looks like: charcoal on rough-grained paper: velvet blacks, smudged greys, with moonlight and fog erased out with an eraser. Hitchcock's *Rebecca* is a black-and-white Gothic dream, the estate first in fog and then in fire, and charcoal can draw both.
- Style: Gothic charcoal drawing
- Keywords: 2D·B&W
- Year: 1940

## Style prompt

Charcoal on toothed paper. Fine tooth (grain ~1/600 of frame width): light pressure catches only the peaks, heavy pressure fills the valleys. Grain only in midtones; velvet blacks, clean-paper lifts. Values: ~60% dark-to-mid grey, 15% deep black in the nearest foreground and largest masses, lights small. Charcoal #121111 on paper #EEECE7. Marks: broken vine lines, side-of-the-stick sweeps (1/60-1/30 of frame width) following form, hatching, finger smudges, eraser lifts for edges and glows; mist is horizontal streaks erased out of a toned area (as in William Kentridge's animated drawings). Only the hero the story centres on is in pastel (e.g. #D97757), laid fairly solid, tooth peeking through, outlined in charcoal; flames stay charcoal-and-paper. Moonlight from above; 2-3 fog bands drift at different speeds. Camera: slow subjective push, foreground parting. Avoid: digital noise, smooth digital glows, grey blacks, empty bright skies.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

vine charcoal, eraser lifts and finger smudging, William Kentridge, Gothic suspense

### Director's notes

Clawd plays the second Mrs. de Winter: velvet hairband, cardigan, gazing up at the estate. It does not appear in the opening dream push-in; then three hard cuts: Clawd → the burning estate → Mrs. Danvers. Every shot pushes inward, with 2.5 seconds driving on toward the great fire.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. A subjective push-in along the overgrown, moonlit drive, the trees parting to reveal Manderley in the fog, a few windows lit that should not be. (0.9s)
2. Close shot of Clawd as the second Mrs. de Winter: velvet hairband, cardigan lapels, Manderley behind her; slow push-in. (0.6s)
3. Manderley under the moon; the camera pushes in as fire kindles in window after window. (0.5s)
4. Mrs. Danvers stands motionless in a burning window, hands folded, her cold face lit flat; the camera pushes in on her face. (0.5s)
