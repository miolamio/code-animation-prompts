## Notes

### Technique keywords

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Raymond: a plaid collar buttoned to the top, several pens and a pencil in the breast pocket. The editing follows the counting: the first few beats circle three piles of toothpicks, and when 246 appears he tilts his head, then hard cuts to Las Vegas, where every card that lands pops up a count marker. The scene plays at 1.1x speed.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. High-angle shot of a black-and-white checkered diner floor: the spilled toothpicks are circled pile by pile with the numbers rolling up as a count, the camera slowly pulling back to bring the 246 at the top into the picture. (1s)
2. Hard cut to a medium close shot of Clawd: plaid shirt collar, a row of pens in the breast pocket, tilting his head to look at the pile of toothpicks on the floor labeled 82, the head tilting lower and lower; the camera slowly pulls back. (0.8s)
3. Hard cut to a Las Vegas blackjack table, shot over the dealer's shoulder: cards fly from the shoe, a Hi-Lo marker and running count popping up beside them; Clawd sits between a Nagel-style woman and Charlie. (0.7s)
