# O25 The Greatest Show on Earth

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Widescreen 1950–1959
- Looks like: Strobridge circus chromolithograph: flat chrome yellow, vermilion and cobalt blue with heavy black outlines, halftone dots, misregistration and stone grain. DeMille's big top is painted as a three-ring poster, with Clawd as Sebastian doing the trapeze catch.
- Style: Strobridge circus chromolithograph
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1952

## Style prompt

A circus chromolithograph after the Strobridge Lithographing Co. (c. 1890). Flat ink, heavy black outlines (about 1/300 of frame width), crescent shadows, light from upper left. Four stones: chrome yellow #F4C416, vermilion #D62C24, cobalt #1E4EA0, key black #181210 on warm cream paper #EBD9B4; overprints and tints give only orange, green, brown, tan, pale blue, grey. Solids print flat; screens (dots about 1/250 of frame width) only on gradients (sky, skin, ground), each stone at its own angle, 1-3 px off register, stone grain. The hero the story centres on is solid yellow under solid vermilion, a flat orange (e.g. #D97757). Exaggerated perspective, radial lines converging on the action, several actions at once; any crowd is tiny individual figures, though a lone figure works. Camera: tilt up, lateral dolly, cut on action. Avoid: off-palette colours, dot-only crowds, a screened, pinkish hero.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

four-color chromolithograph, Strobridge circus poster, flat ink + heavy black outline

### Director's notes

Clawd plays The Great Sebastian: a sequined cape with a gold scalloped edge, hanging upside down from the trapeze. A tilt up finds it on the trapeze bar; the elephant in the middle moves laterally so the parade keeps going forward; and finally it cuts mid-action to the catch, timed to the point where the trapeze swings to the right, matching the poster composition, as it flings an arm toward the audience.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Wide shot of the big top: three rings, bowl-shaped seating, elephants on the track; tilt up the tent to Clawd hanging upside down from the highest trapeze. (0.8s)
2. A lateral tracking shot of the elephant parade: each trunk curled around the tail ahead, sequined dancers riding their backs, with the railing and the backs of front-row spectators' heads in the foreground. (0.6s)
3. Cut mid-action to the catch: Clawd in a sequined cape hangs by the crook of its knees, one hand gripping a flyer in pink, the other flung wide to the audience, the trapeze swinging them to the right; the camera pushes in slightly and tilts a little. (1s)
