# O3 All Quiet on the Western Front

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Silent and Early Sound 1927–1938
- Looks like: a Kollwitz-style woodcut: large masses of black, white contours cut with a knife, rough wood grain, with Clawd overprinted from a separate orange block. Kollwitz's anti-war prints of 1921-22 come from the same war and the same grief as this film.
- Style: Kollwitz-style anti-war woodcut
- Keywords: 2D·B&W
- Year: 1929/30

## Style prompt

Expressionist woodcut after Käthe Kollwitz, *Krieg* (1921-22). 60-70% solid black; white contour cuts 1/300-1/150 of frame width separate forms; faces are white shapes with a few black features; hands large. Figures fill the frame, cropped by its edges. Each cut tapers in and out with chipped edges, is wider the nearer it sits to the light, in slightly uneven bundles. Ink #17130F on paper #E8DFCA; texture is only wood-grain streaks and under-inked speckle (±5%). One colour block (e.g. #D97757) only on the hero the story centres on, flat on its body or clothes, cuts over it; black stays 60%+. Light enters through one opening (window, cloud gap); lamps get a short ring of cuts. The ground under figures stays uncut black. Quiet motion (breathing, a small gesture); slow pans and push-ins. Avoid: grey fog or midtones, even striped hatching, gore.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

woodcut print, Käthe Kollwitz, German Expressionism, two-block overprint

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Paul, in a German army helmet. The whole piece is slowed down (1.2x) and kept very quiet: around beat 3 he begins to reach out, taking three beats to reach all the way; the ghost soldiers appear only in the last shot. Four hard cuts: faces in the trench → Paul's arm → the butterfly → the cemetery of crosses.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. A pan along the trench sweeping across carved faces: one with hands over its face, one staring in terror, one asleep. (0.7s)
2. A face under a helmet looking straight into the lens (a nod to the original 1930 poster); slow push-in. (0.4s)
3. Medium shot: Clawd in a German helmet stands on the firing step, slowly reaching his arm toward the loophole, where a white butterfly rests on a rusty can at the edge of the hole; the camera pushes in. (0.9s)
4. Close-up of the white butterfly at the loophole, Clawd's orange arm reaching in from the left; push in. (0.7s)
5. Big wide shot: no man's land and a cemetery of white crosses under flare light, a column of ghost soldiers fading in and walking off into the distance; slow pull back. (0.6s)
