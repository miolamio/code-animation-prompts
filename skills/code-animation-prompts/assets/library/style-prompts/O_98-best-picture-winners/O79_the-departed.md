# O79 The Departed

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · New Millennium 2000–2009
- Looks like: Flat multi-layer spray-paint stencils with overspray fuzz and drips, paint sinking into the brick joints, a torn wheat-pasted poster beside it. The last shot of The Departed is a rat crawling along the railing beneath the golden dome of the State House, and street stencil graffiti has always loved painting rats.
- Style: Boston red-brick wall stencil graffiti
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 2006

## Style prompt

Street stencil graffiti on an old red-brick wall. Each piece is 3–5 stencils sprayed as flat colour, photo-derived in look (smallest cut detail about 1/200 of frame width): hard cut edges, soft overspray where a stencil lifted, spray dots, drips, stencil bridges. Palette: black #151312, mid grey #8C8A85, gold #C99A2E, red #C3261F; brick #7A3224, cream painted patches under the main pieces. Brick relief, mortar and rain streaks show through, so paint sinks into the joints. A busy wall: several pieces side by side, short hand-sprayed slogans about the subject (user's language), old tags, a torn halftone paste-up. Light: soft raking daylight, mild contrast, a faint swaying wire shadow; no heavy vignette. Camera: pieces spray on layer by layer, then close-ups and lateral tracks. Reference: Banksy. Avoid: clean vector edges; paint floating on the brick; too many colours; a dark wall.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Multi-layer spray-paint stencils, Banksy's rats, red brick wall texture, halftone wheat-paste poster

### Director's notes

Clawd plays the undercover cop, a flip phone held to his ear, and is himself one more layer sprayed onto the wall. The first two beats spray the piece on layer by layer; after that the phone, the rat and Clawd hanging up come in rapid cuts. Nobody speaks; the ringtone and the rat tell the story.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Wide shot: the whole wall, with the stencil piece sprayed on layer by layer: the city, the golden dome, the railing, the rat, Clawd holding a phone, a red slogan; slight push-in. (0.8s)
2. Close shot: two spray-painted flip phones ring across the gap, with a phone against Clawd's ear too; he listens, his eyes darting left; the camera slowly drifts right. (0.6s)
3. Medium shot: the rat runs along the railing beneath the golden dome, "WHO'S THE RAT?" sprayed at upper left, red and blue police light sweeping across the brick wall; the camera tracks right with it. (0.6s)
4. Close shot: Clawd lowers the phone and looks to the right; slight push-in. (0.4s)
