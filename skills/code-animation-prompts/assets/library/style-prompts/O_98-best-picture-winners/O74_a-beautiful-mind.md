# O74 A Beautiful Mind

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · New Millennium 2000–2009
- Looks like: Handwritten math on two surfaces: white grease pencil on the glass of a Gothic library window and chalk on a blackboard, with the numbers lighting up and joined by gold lines. This is how Nash sees patterns in the film: it is light in his head, not a computer interface.
- Style: formulas on a window and chalk on a blackboard + golden connecting lines
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 2001

## Style prompt


---

English:

Handwriting as the image: translucent white wax-marker strokes (about 1/300 of frame width) appear stroke by stroke on a window of square leaded panes: formulas, figures or notes about the subject, in small blocks on a quarter of the glass. Outside, the subject's world, soft-focus, mid-toned and warm (overcast gold, muted green), never brighter than the writing. A warm interior (shelves, green-shaded lamp, amber light) frames the window. At the key moment, chosen symbols brighten and soft gold lines join them into a star or polygon, lights running along. Variant: chalk on green slate with ghost writing. Palette: slate green #2F3E35, chalk white #F1EFE6, lamp amber #E0A050, soft gold #D9B45A. Camera: slow push-ins, each cut closer to the writing. Reference: cinematographer Roger Deakins (*A Beautiful Mind*, 2001). Avoid: HUD graphics; typed fonts; writing filling the glass; cold blue outside.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

White grease pencil on glass, blackboard chalk, glowing gold connecting lines, Deakins-style window light

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Nash: an open shirt collar, a loosened knit tie, a grease pencil in hand at the window. The first shot is writing, the next two are light; each hard cut moves closer to the numbers, and in the end Clawd leaves the frame and only the pattern remains.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Medium wide shot: in front of a Gothic library window, Clawd as Nash stands on the window seat writing game theory formulas on the glass with a white grease pencil, an autumn quadrangle outside; slow push-in. (1s)
2. Close shot: handwriting on the glass, the view outside out of focus, Clawd's pen visible at lower left; the characters light up and gold lines join them into a star; slight push-in. (0.8s)
3. Close shot: groups of five-digit code numbers on a blackboard, the numbers lighting up one by one, and a golden seven-pointed star pattern drawing itself; slight push-in. No Clawd. (0.8s)
