# O8 Mutiny on the Bounty

## Style prompt

A late-18th-century copperplate map, default an Admiralty sea chart (inland: a survey map). Fine burin hatching and cross-hatching (lines ~1/1200 of frame width) give all tone; a minimum line width makes small detail darken, not vanish. Ink #1C1610 on laid paper #EFE3C4, light foxing, folds. Thin hand colour: water #9EBCBC along edges, land #B8C08C; the hero the story centres on (e.g. #D97757) is the one strong colour. Rhumb lines from 32-point roses, a graduated border, hachured hills, cartouche, one ornament (e.g. a sea monster), serif lettering. Put the action in an unboxed engraved vignette fading into the chart, filling 60-75% of frame height at the end of the move. Camera: one 5-6 s glide from the whole sheet into the vignette, chart still around it; a dotted route inks itself in. Avoid: cropping the paper, heavy washes, stiff figures.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

18th-century Admiralty sea chart, copperplate line engraving, hand-applied light wash, Robert Dodd's prints

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Fletcher Christian: a bicorne hat worn sideways, holding a cutlass. The whole piece never leaves the chart; a single shot glides in, with no cuts. On beat 4 he raises the blade, followed at once by a flash along the blade pointing at the launch; over the last few beats the launch drifts away astern.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. One continuous take: starting from the whole chart, the Bounty advances along a dotted track and the camera glides into the ship illustration on the chart, rhumb lines and compass roses still all around; on deck Clawd raises his cutlass and points at Bligh to order him into the boat, the launch drops astern, and the track toward Timor is inked in across the chart as it goes. (2.5s)
