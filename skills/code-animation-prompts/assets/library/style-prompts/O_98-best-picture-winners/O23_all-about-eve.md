## Style prompt

An Al Hirschfeld-style caricature. Lines are elastic, long and continuous, one stroke per contour: about 1/800 of frame width at the ends, swelling to 1/250 on curves. Dark clothing, hair and big objects go solid black; grey only as sparse parallel hatching. The setting is fully drawn in thin line, architecture, furniture and props in detail, white kept between objects: about 60% paper white, never an empty page. Push one trait per figure (a long neck, a big head). Paper #FBFAF6, ink #131012; only the hero the story centres on has its body or main garment filled flat with one colour (e.g. #D97757) instead of black. Lines write on within the first second, then hold; a short hand-lettered line may sit in the white. Camera: ease back, pan across, push in. Avoid: stiff limbs, stick figures, a bare setting.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Margo: a row of short black curls, a strand of pearls, a martini, one hand resting on the banister. The first shot is the picture being drawn and the line being written; a pan finds Eve in the corner, staring at her; after the final hard cut, Margo raises her glass on the beat. Performance at 1.8x speed.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. A wide shot of Margo's party is drawn stroke by stroke: first the staircase, then the guests and the piano; "FASTEN YOUR SEATBELTS, IT'S GOING TO BE A BUMPY NIGHT" is handwritten in the white space, with Clawd as Margo standing on the staircase; the camera pulls back slightly. (1.1s)
2. A pan right sweeps over the guests: Birdie, Max, Addison, Miss Caswell, the pianist, stopping on Eve in the corner. (0.8s)
3. Close shot of Clawd on the staircase: black curls, a pearl necklace, raising a martini; gentle push-in. (0.7s)
