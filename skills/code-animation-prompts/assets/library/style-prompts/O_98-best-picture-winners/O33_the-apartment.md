- Looks like: Saul Bass-style white lines slide in over black to build the film's most famous one-point-perspective endless office hall, then fill in with gray tones. Bass made this film's title sequence; the only warm color in the whole frame is Clawd and the key tag.

## Style prompt

Saul Bass title lines turned into a set. A shot opens on black: thin white lines (about 1/800 of frame width) slide in and grow into a grid; by 2–3 s grey planes fill in and lights switch on row by row. Then hold the finished frame 3 s: mid-to-light grey, flat fills in 4–5 even steps from #140F0D to #F4F1E8, every shape outlined in dark line about 1/600 of frame width. Strict one-point perspective, centred vanishing point; identical elements repeat into depth (desks and people, or posts, lamps, windows). Figures: flat two-tone shapes. The only warm colour is orange-red #E2472A, for the one hero subject and one prop; all light stays white. Camera: a fast push to the hero, a hard cut to top-down, the grid becoming pattern. Avoid: sketchy lines, gradients, colour leaks, skewed perspective, unfilled wireframe.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Saul Bass title design, one-point-perspective office hall, black-and-white film grain, a single warm color

### Director's notes

Clawd plays C.C. Baxter: a bowler hat and an apartment key with an orange-red tag (51 W 67), sitting on the edge of a front-row desk. The office hall is drawn line by line; on beat 2 it holds up the key and the camera pushes in sharply; the last cut goes to an overhead view of the grid of desks, a graphic match with the grid of Manhattan blocks in the next part's opening overhead shot.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. One continuous take: white lines build an endless office hall out of total black, gray tones and rows of ceiling lights coming on row by row; Clawd (Baxter), sitting on the edge of a front-row desk, holds up the apartment key on beat 2 and the camera pushes in sharply. (1.9s)
2. Hard cut to an overhead view straight down: the desks form a grid, Clawd lying on its back on its own desk with the key and tag in its hand; slow push-in. (0.6s)
