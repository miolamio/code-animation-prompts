# O12 Gone with the Wind

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Color and War 1939–1949
- Looks like: impasto oil painting: palette-knife color blocks, ridges of piled paint, the heat of Technicolor - skies burning from gold to blood red, red earth, black silhouettes. Full color arrives with *Gone with the Wind*, and its first fully colored image is Tara at sunset.
- Style: Technicolor impasto oil painting
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1939

## Style prompt


---

English:

Impasto oil in a 1939 Technicolor palette. Palette-knife slabs: long horizontal sky bands (1/15-1/6 of frame width long, about 1/60 tall), shorter strokes following form on land and figures, calm thin areas between; ridges lit from upper left, paint thickest in highlights, canvas weave where thin. Default sunset sky: wine #3A0912, red #A8241A, orange #F27A22, gold #FFD878; ground red-brown #6E2214; warm near-black silhouettes. The hero the story centres on stays a readable warm mid-tone (e.g. #D97757), never a silhouette. Strong backlight, gold rims; in close shots a warm side light on half the face and hands. Epic-poster composition: big dark silhouettes (trees, buildings, rocks, figures) on a low horizon. Lamps as painted flames. Camera: slow push on the wide, low push-in on the hero, crane up to a vista. Avoid: tiled same-size strokes, muddy browns, smooth gradients.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

impasto oil painting, palette knife, 1939 Technicolor palette, epic poster painting

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Scarlett: a wide-brimmed straw hat with a green satin ribbon, holding a radish. Over the first 7 beats the sky burns from gold to blood red, and on beat 6 the radish is raised. Four hard cuts run through Tara, the vow, Atlanta and the depot, and the last 5 beats are one long crane shot.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Wide silhouette of Tara: a great oak, the white house and cedars stand dark and heavy beneath a sky burning from gold to blood red, with Clawd on a mound of red earth holding a radish; slow push-in. (1.7s)
2. Push in with the horizon pressed low, the burning sky behind: Clawd in the wide straw hat with a green satin ribbon holds the radish high, ringed in a gold rim of backlight. (1s)
3. Close shot of Clawd's face and the straw hat, the ribbon tails streaming in the wind. (0.4s)
4. The burning of Atlanta: a wagon dashes past burning warehouses and timber frames, Clawd on the driver's seat next to Rhett, with Prissy and Melanie behind; the camera tracks laterally while tongues of flame sweep through the foreground. (1.5s)
5. One long crane shot at the railway depot: rising and pulling back from Clawd standing among the wounded, rows of wounded men covering the red earth all the way to the horizon, a tattered flag in the middle, Clawd shrinking to a small orange dot. (2.1s)
