## Style prompt


---

English:

A real 3D scene in late-1940s deep-focus black-and-white. Built forms: bare masonry, block by block, bevelled, staggered (Romanesque arches only where the setting has them); wet surfaces dark and glossy. Soft overcast light, no hard shadows: dark masses against a silver sky. Low fog fills the lower third; the far background ends at 60-70% grey, the midground sharp; a bright patch behind cloud throws faint shafts. Water, if any: layered swells, lace foam, misty spray. Post: ambient occlusion, gentle bloom, high-contrast silver grade (true blacks, unclipped whites), grain (±3%), faint flicker. Only the hero the story centres on keeps its colour (e.g. #D97757); every light source stays grey. Camera: slow drift in, a track along a high edge, a low angle on the hero. Reference: Laurence Olivier, *Hamlet* (1948). Avoid: hard sunlit shadows, white-out fog, tiled textures.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

three.js 3D + volumetric fog, Desmond Dickinson deep-focus black-and-white, Roger Furse's bare Romanesque sets

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Hamlet: Yorick's skull, and the white lace cuffs, belt and short sword from the poster. Three hard cuts run through three images from the film: the castle on the cliff, the ghost in the fog, the soliloquy rock; in the last shot the skull is raised on the beat. Performance at 1.8x speed.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Wide shot from the sea: Elsinore on its headland with surf breaking at its foot, Clawd a small figure on top of the rock at right; the camera drifts slowly closer. (0.7s)
2. The rampart walk in thick fog: an armored ghost takes shape out of the mist and beckons, Clawd watching it from close by; gentle push-in. (0.8s)
3. Low-angle shot of the rock by the sea: Clawd, in lace cuffs with a short sword at his side, raises Yorick's skull in front of the castle in the fog; slow push-in. (1.1s)
