## Notes

### Technique keywords

1850s cased daguerreotype, hand-tinted tintype, studio still life

### Director's notes

No costume, and no person drawn: Clawd stands beside Solomon's violin and slowly bows his head. The whole sequence has just three movements: the image developing, one slow push-in, the head lowering, in 2.5 seconds with no cut.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Single continuous take: it opens on the whole camera case on a table, the daguerreotype plate blank; the picture develops in from one side (a violin leaning against a ladder-back chair, Clawd standing beside it), and the camera slowly pushes in until the plate fills the frame as Clawd lowers his head. (2.5s)
