# O90 The Shape of Water

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Indie Era 2010–2019
- Looks like: A flooded bathroom and laboratory tank in real-time 3D, the whole picture soaked in teal: flowing caustics, shafts of light falling from above, bubbles and a few warm lamps. Del Toro's film is steeped in this kind of water, so the whole sequence is shot underwater.
- Style: 3D underwater teal caustics
- Keywords: 3D
- Year: 2017

## Style prompt

Real-time 3D, fully under water (land subjects sink into a flooded room or sunken place), in teal: background #041414, hemisphere light #5fb3a6 above and #0c2a24 below; teal fog, reds fading first with distance. A strong source above the surface projects moving caustics: bright cells about 1/20 of frame width on near surfaces, sliding over floor, walls and the subject. Shafts fan down; edges stay dark. Bubbles, motes, the rippled surface from below. Warm gold on 1–3 practicals, at least one large (a lit window or lamp ~1/10 of frame width), plus cyan bioluminescence. Smooth-shaded models, ambient occlusion, shallow focus, modest bloom, grain ±3%. Signature shot: low angle looking up, the subject half the frame height, off-centre beside the light; slow push-ins. Reference: Guillermo del Toro, *The Shape of Water* (2017). Avoid: flat water, caustics as sky stripes, tiny warm accents.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Real-time 3D (three.js), projected caustics and volumetric light shafts, Dan Laustsen's teal and warm gold grade

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Elisa, with a deep red velvet headband and bow on her head and later a hard-boiled egg in hand. Four cuts in 2.5 seconds with a steady pace at 1.25x: the embrace under the light shaft, the faces pressed together, the tank, and finally two hands, with a teal glow lighting up at the point of contact as the closing beat.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Low-position wide shot in the flooded bathroom: the amphibian man holds Clawd in his arms under a light shaft, a red shoe sinking; slow push-in. (0.9s)
2. Cut to a close shot of faces: his crest and gills pressed against a corner of Clawd, the red headband bow, bubbles rising; the camera slowly pushes in. (0.5s)
3. Cut to the laboratory: medium shot, Clawd standing before the glowing tank holding a hard-boiled egg, reaching back to press a hand against the glass; slow push-in. (0.6s)
4. Cut to a close-up: two hands pressed together through the glass, a ball of teal light glowing at the point of contact. (0.4s)
