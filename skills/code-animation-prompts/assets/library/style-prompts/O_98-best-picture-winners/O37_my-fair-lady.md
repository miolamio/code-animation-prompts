# O37 My Fair Lady

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · The Swinging Sixties 1960–1969
- Looks like: quick dry-brush ink lines with pink and magenta watercolor washes and flicked spatters, dissolving into the white paper at the edges. The 1964 original poster was Bob Peak's ink line with pastel color; the scene is Ascot in black and white, designed by Cecil Beaton.
- Style: Bob Peak fashion illustration
- Keywords: 2D

## Style prompt

1960s fashion illustration after Bob Peak. Fast ink line, thicker in turns, dry-brush tail, a flick off; 1/600–1/250 of frame width, thinning on tight curves. Solid black for dark clothing, hats and stripes; faces bare paper #FBF7F1. Loose, soft-edged watercolour pools, darker at drying edges: pink #EE84B2 as the main field, magenta #CE3076 for hot spots, rose #F4B2CC, a touch of plum #80285C; warm grey for secondary figures and shading; directional splatter. Settings are a few lines in these washes, not literal colour. The frieze is essential: slender profile figures 9–10 heads tall repeated across the frame (the hero plus grey-wash figures or echoes), black-and-white pattern over pink; washes cover 60–80% of the frame. Camera: slow drift along the figures; graphic-mask transitions. Avoid: even-width outlines, flat digital fills, polygon-edged washes, a lone figure on an empty page. Line thins on straights.

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Eliza: an enormous white hat (striped satin ribbon, roses, a white ostrich feather), with a ring of lace at the neckline and skirt hem. The opening lifts the hat brim out of the black of the previous part, and the ending closes into the two circles of the crowd's binoculars, in which the next part, *The Sound of Music*, opens. Three shots, an unhurried rhythm.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Out of black, the brim of the big Ascot hat lifts to reveal a close shot of Clawd (Eliza); it turns its gaze toward the camera and dips a slight curtsy, the picture easing back lightly. (0.8s)
2. Wide shot of Ascot: Clawd stands before two rows of stiff aristocrats in Beaton's black-and-white costumes; slow lateral move as it lifts its chin. (0.6s)
3. A pan along the rigid crowd, everyone raising their binoculars at once, and finally the frame closes into two binocular circles. (1.1s)
