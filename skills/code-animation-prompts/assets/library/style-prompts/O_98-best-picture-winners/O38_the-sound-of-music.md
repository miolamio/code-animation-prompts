## Style prompt


---

English:

Storybook 3D landscape in saturated 1960s widescreen colour: smooth sculpted forms, not low-poly or photoreal. Default light: bright sun high, a little behind the camera: emerald grass #3FA33A, cobalt sky #2F6FD0, clean white snow and clouds. Rounded ridges, snow lines following slope, grey-ochre rock bands, cloud shadows. Haze only on the farthest ridges, about 25% lilac. Cumulus are lit white cotton puffs. Knee-high grass and wildflowers fill the foreground, bending in gusts. A lower sun lengthens shadows and warms only cloud edges; other settings keep these colours. Camera: one continuous move: 0–2 s high flight, 2–4 s descent, then a low orbit through the grass around the subject (about 20° per second, subject about 1/4 of frame height). References: cinematographer Ted McCord (*The Sound of Music*, 1965); Miyazaki's meadows. Avoid: violet wash, needle peaks, grey photoreal rock, static grass.

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Maria: an edelweiss and a small headscarf knot on its head, holding a striped carpet bag in one hand and a guitar case in the other, as on the poster. The twirl is one continuous curve running through three shots, and every cut lands halfway through the turn; on the last beat both arms go high.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. The picture opens to full screen out of the two binocular circles of the previous part: a high aerial shot sweeping past snow peaks through cotton-ball cumulus clouds. (0.7s)
2. Descending toward the grassy summit: a tiny Clawd dead center in the frame, already spinning with the carpet bag and guitar case flung out, a patchwork of forest and fields in the distance; the camera descends toward it. (0.8s)
3. Low camera position skimming the grass, circling Clawd as it completes a full turn, the bag and case swinging wide, snow peaks and cumulus behind; at the end it throws both arms high. (1s)
