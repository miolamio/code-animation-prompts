## Style prompt


---

English:

New Yorker single-panel cartoon, full-bleed, no border. Nib-pen lines about 1/1000 of frame width on straights, swelling to 1/300 in turns, flicking off at the ends; box corners overlap. Pale watercolour washes with pooled edges over most objects, slipping past the lines by at most 1/150 of frame width: grey #9698A0, sea blue #78A0BE, sand #E2CCA0, rust #C45C32, on cold-press paper #FBF8F1 fading to white at the edges. Exaggerated, expressive figures with drawn faces among everyday props. One hand-lettered caption line under the action, in the user's language, about 1/30 of frame height, appearing letter by letter: a deadpan remark. The drawing holds still; living things wobble. Camera: small drifts and pushes; the frame may split into two side-by-side drawings. References: James Thurber, William Steig, Jean-Jacques Sempé. Avoid: saturated colour, uniform vector lines, a small framed panel floating in white.

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Annie: Diane Keaton's wide-brimmed hat, a slouchy striped tie and vest, and a 35mm camera in hand. About 1.8x speed, dropping to near 1x on the jump: two flash photos, the lobster crawls to its feet, it jumps in fright, then lands laughing. All hard cuts, ending with a split-screen dinner table as a graphic exit.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Big close-up of the gap behind the fridge: a lobster slowly pokes its head out; slow camera drift. (0.6s)
2. Wide shot of the kitchen: Clawd as Annie laughs while taking flash photos of Alvy holding a stockpot; the lobster crawls to its feet, it jumps, then lands laughing, with "TALK TO HIM. YOU SPEAK SHELLFISH." written stroke by stroke below; the camera pulls back slightly with a touch of handheld shake. (1.5s)
3. Easter dinner table in split screen: the quiet Hall household on the left, with Clawd seated among them; the noisy Singer household on the right. (0.4s)
