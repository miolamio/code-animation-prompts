## Style prompt

Three-ink Risograph: orange-red #EC5032 as the dominant field (about 60%, with drip streaks), deep blue #162C76 for silhouettes and shadows, fluorescent pink #FF48B0 only for rims and halos, on warm paper #F7F2E7. Each ink is halftoned at its own angle, dots 1/300 of frame width, plates misregistered 2–4 px; grain, pinholes, slight boil. Knock orange out under blue silhouettes so they print pure blue; only the darkest accents overprint to near-black. Light is the absence of ink: beams, lamps and lit ground show bare paper, ringed in pink. Figures: blue silhouettes, pink rims; skin is 30–60% orange with a blue shadow step. At cooler times keep the red field; blue rises only into the top of the sky. Camera: handheld behind someone carrying a light, a reverse close-up, a push. Avoid: blue-dominated frames, smooth gradients, a fourth ink, clean registration.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Virgil Tibbs: white shirt collar points, a dark narrow tie, shirt cuffs, a suitcase at its feet. The candlelight snuffed out in the previous part carries the night in. Three shots build the confrontation: flashlights find it, the sheriff stops chewing his gum, and it draws itself up straight, as if saying "They call me MISTER Tibbs!"

### Storyboard in the final film

1. A handheld flashlight POV in the dark: the deputy's beam sweeps the station platform and finds Clawd (Tibbs) beside the suitcase; it looks up with wide eyes, and several more flashlights come on. (0.8s)
2. Reverse close shot of Sheriff Gillespie: peaked cap, aviator sunglasses, chewing gum, then stopping; slow push-in. (0.5s)
3. Mid-wide shot of the standoff: Clawd stands between the deputy and the sheriff, several flashlight beams crossing on it, the blue police car and the Sparta water tower behind; it draws up straight and squares its shoulders as the camera pushes in. (1.2s)
