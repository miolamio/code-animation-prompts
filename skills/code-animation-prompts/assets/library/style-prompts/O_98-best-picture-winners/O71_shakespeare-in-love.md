## Style prompt

Elizabethan portrait miniature in Nicholas Hilliard's manner: opaque watercolour laid flat on warm vellum, very fine hatching in shadows (lines about 1/1000 of frame width), lead-white dots on lit edges, red-brown outlines. Palette: ultramarine ground #203E92, shell gold #D6B05C (granular flecks, a few burnished highlights), carmine #A8202C, lead white #F6F2E8, verdigris #346C40. Signature shot: the picture inside a gold-rimmed oval locket lying on rust-red damask; a flowering white eglantine briar fills the oval behind and around the subject; a short motto in gold Roman capitals arcs along the top. The oval's ground stays ultramarine; landscape and sky go in as flat colour bands. Wide scenes may leave the locket, same method, true perspective. Slow push, slight tilt; only flowers, cloth and gestures move. Reference: Hilliard, *Young Man Among Roses* (c. 1587). Avoid: airbrush gradients; thick oil strokes; flat yellow gold.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Court limning miniature portraits, opaque watercolor on parchment, shell gold, crimson brocade still life

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Shakespeare: a starched ruff collar and a gold earring, holding a quill in the miniature and a white rose on the stage. Four shots hard cut, running from the private miniature to the public theater, with the Queen slowly nodding on the last beat to close the sequence.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Close shot: a gold pendant miniature on crimson brocade, with Clawd as Shakespeare standing among white wild roses in a ruff collar, a quill writing; the camera slowly pushes in with a slight tilt down. (0.8s)
2. Wide shot: the Rose Theatre. The camera tilts down from the painted canopy and the galleries above and pushes in to the stage, where Clawd stands holding a white rose, and the Queen sits in the box on the right. (0.6s)
3. Medium shot: the balcony scene, Juliet leaning her chin on her hand above, Clawd below raising a white rose to her; slight push-in. (0.6s)
4. Close shot: Queen Elizabeth in her box under the canopy, turning her face from the play, fanning herself and slowly nodding; slight push-in. (0.6s)
