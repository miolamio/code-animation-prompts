# O64 The Silence of the Lambs

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Turn of the Century 1990–1999
- Looks like: Fine copperplate hatching and stippling with a thin layer of hand coloring on top, plate numbers and Latin labels printed on yellowed paper. The death's-head hawkmoth on the poster belongs on a specimen plate, and the same cold copperplate treatment carries on into Lecter's cell.
- Style: hand-colored natural history copperplate engraving
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1991

## Style prompt

A hand-coloured natural-history copperplate. Every object in engraved warm black-brown line: dense parallel and cross-hatching (spacing about 1/400 of frame width, thicker in darks), stipple for soft tones, hatched shadows beneath; sky bare or sparsely lined. Flat watercolour tints inside the line, medium saturation, clearly coloured: paper #EFE3C4, umber #5A3A22, rust #B4431E, yellow #E6AD24, grey-violet #7D6F8E. Plate: the subject as Fig. 1 in the centre, about 60% of plate height, 3–6 numbered part or object figures around it, each about 1/5 high; spaced small-caps title, numbered legend, bevelled plate mark, light foxing. Even daylight; time of day only shifts the tints' warmth; dark interiors go cold, hard side light. Camera: a still plate where one thing moves (insect, leaf, hand), then a slow push to a key detail. Reference: Moses Harris, *The Aurelian* (1766). Avoid: smooth gradients; garish colour; a tiny vignetted subject. The title sits above the plate and the legend below. The push is at constant speed.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Copperplate engraved hatching, hand coloring, Victorian entomology plates, Moses Harris's The Aurelian

### Director's notes

Clawd is first "Fig. 4" on the plate, then plays Clarice with an FBI trainee ID pinned on. The moth lands in the first few beats; hard cut to the cell, and the push-in on Lecter is one unbroken shot, his smile saved for the very end. Scene time is about 1.8x screen time.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. A full entomological plate, "Acherontia styx": a living death's-head hawkmoth lifts off a nightshade plant and lands on top of Clawd's head; gentle push-in. (0.9s)
2. Hard cut to the underground ward: Clawd (Clarice) with an FBI trainee ID pinned on stands before the glass; then one continuous push-in moves right and inward until Lecter's face fills the frame, a hint of a smile at the corner of his mouth. (1.6s)
