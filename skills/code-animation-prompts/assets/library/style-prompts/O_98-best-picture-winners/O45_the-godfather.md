# O45 The Godfather

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · New Hollywood 1970–1979
- Looks like: oil painting in deep amber and umber, warm only where the overhead light falls: shadows are thin transparent glazes, lit edges are impasto. It recreates the overhead light of Gordon Willis in that study where "I believe in America" is spoken.
- Style: Rembrandt overhead-light chiaroscuro oil painting
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1972

## Style prompt


---

English:

Chiaroscuro oil painting after Rembrandt and Caravaggio, one overhead light. About 70% of the frame is readable warm dark, mostly umber #24150B, near-black #070403 only in the deepest corners. Amber #8F5D2A mid-tones about 20%; top planes reach #E2B673. The hero stands in the light pool, lit area at least 1/4 of frame height; brows shade the eyes. One red accent #9C1A1A. Darks thin smooth glazes; lights thick impasto, ridged strokes about 1/100 of frame width; modelling soft, never scratchy. Amber varnish, heavy vignette, canvas grain (±3%). A warm bright opening (window, doorway, horizon) may glow behind; a small lamp in frame is a small glow, not a second key. Camera: slow pull-backs and pushes. Reference: Francis Ford Coppola, *The Godfather* (1972), cinematographer Gordon Willis. Avoid: lifting the darks, flat light, faces popping out, frames too dark to read.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

chiaroscuro oil painting, Gordon Willis's overhead light, Rembrandt and Caravaggio glazing

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Don Corleone: a black bow tie, a wing collar, a red rose, white cuffs, one hand resting on a cat. All 12 beats run at 1x speed, keeping Willis's patient shots; the stroking of the cat takes about 4 seconds, with one blink in the middle. All hard cuts, and in the last shot the puppeteer's hand from the original poster descends onto its head.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Starting from Bonasera's gray hair in the dark, the camera pulls out slowly to reveal the Godfather in a wingback chair with the cat on his lap. (1.7s)
2. Close shot: Clawd under the overhead light slowly strokes a gray tabby cat, the bow tie and red rose catching the light; slow push-in. (1s)
3. Push toward the venetian blinds: the wedding in sunlight outside the window, hazy and overexposed, with Hagen's silhouette at left. (0.8s)
4. Wide shot of the study, with a slight push-in: the puppet-master's hand lowers from the top of the frame, the strings slowly tightening on Clawd. (1.5s)
