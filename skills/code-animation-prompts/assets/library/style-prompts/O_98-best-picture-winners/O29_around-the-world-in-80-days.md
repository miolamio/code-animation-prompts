## Style prompt


---

English:

1950s UPA modernist cartoon. Uneven ink lines #221C19 about 1/400 of frame width, sometimes broken; colour blocks sit about 1/300 of frame width off them, all one way. Objects are overlapping flat cut-out facets: a lit face and a shadow face one flat step (about 15%) darker. Pale aqua sky and cream paper #F2E9D3 cover most of the frame; mustard #E3A92B, teal #2B8C86, orange-red #D4452B are accents; sky, greens and slate blues are these mixed toward paper. Other times of day change only the sky colour. Crayon grain ±5% brightness everywhere, few flecks. Poster layout: one big graphic shape (a building or sun disc), angular outlined figures beside it. Light beams: flat pale wedges. Hard cuts every 1–2 s; slow parallax drift. After UPA, *Gerald McBoing-Boing* (1950); Saul Bass, *Around the World in 80 Days* titles (1956). Avoid: gradients, perfect registration, saturated colour everywhere.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

UPA limited animation, Saul Bass end-title animation, 1956 original poster palette, crayon-grain printing

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Fogg: a top hat, a furled umbrella, a glass of champagne. A hard cut every two beats: map, wide shot, close shot. A red route circles the globe, a balloon drifts over the Alps, and the close shot lands on Passepartout scooping snow from a snowy peak to chill the champagne.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. An insert of a map, establishing the camera position: a globe turning on a teal ground while a red route runs in turn through London, Suez, Bombay, Yokohama and San Francisco; Clawd sits in a small balloon at right. (0.9s)
2. Wide shot: a mustard-yellow balloon with a blue checkerboard band drifts over the faceted Alps and a Swiss town, Clawd in a top hat standing at the edge of the basket; slow lateral move. (0.8s)
3. Close shot of Clawd as Fogg: top hat, furled umbrella, holding up a champagne glass, while beside him Passepartout leans out to scoop snow from the snowy peak with an ice bucket; slight push-in. (0.8s)
