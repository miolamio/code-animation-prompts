- Looks like: A polished sperm whale tooth on an oak table, engraved with fine black lines and touches of red, blue and yellow: Gloucester fishing boats, a family hauling in nets, Ruby singing at the bow. It is the folk craft of the New England coast where the film is set.

## Style prompt


---

English:

Scrimshaw: 19th-century sailor's engraving on a whale tooth. Signature shot: a broad, flat-sided tooth lies horizontal on an oak table, about 85% of frame width and 70% of its height; ink bottle, jackknife beside. Engrave in near-black #17110c: needle hatching (lines 1/800 of frame width, spaced about 1/500), swelling outlines, stipple. Lines cover ~70% of the face, dense in sea, subject and land, sky lightly hatched; seas as rows of stylised waves. The hero is large (~1/3 of the tooth's height), coloured; otherwise red #9a2a22, blue #2b4a78, yellow #e9b526 only as small touches. Warm ivory, growth bands, scratches, honey tip. Folk layout: rope border, ribbon motto, rayed sun, motifs from the subject's world. Shade the edges, one gloss streak; don't warp the picture. Push in, then pan slowly as waves animate. Avoid: thick lines, solid fills, a slim horn.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

19th-century whalers' scrimshaw, engraving-knife hatching and stippling, tabletop still-life product lighting

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Ruby, in a yellow waterproof fisherman's hat, standing on a fish box at the bow. Just one cut: from the object into the engraving, then a single gentle lateral move that carries the eye from the father's sign language to the singing, with a musical staff unfurling along with the song.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Wide shot: the engraved whale tooth on an oak table beside a folding knife and an ink bottle; the camera pushes in until the tooth fills the frame. (0.8s)
2. Cut into the engraving: a medium wide shot of the fishing boat, the camera slowly moving laterally from left to right with a slight push-in, from Frank hauling in the net while signing "I love you", to Clawd singing at the bow, with a line of staff unrolling from his raised arm. (1.7s)
