## Style prompt


---

English:

Painterly interiors where the wall carries the image (outdoor subjects move into a related room with a window). Primary: a diamond-tufted padded wall in gold #C9A050 over deep brown #3A2610, brass button highlights, a warm glow behind the sound source (e.g. a hanging microphone). Secondary, for quiet scenes: a cool grey-green wall worn through to blue, pink, ochre and plaster, with damp lines and fine cracks; patches irregular, under a third of the wall. Clean-edged shapes under a subtle brush texture (strokes ~1/120 of frame width, ±5% brightness); faces, small objects crisp. Sound: 4–8 thin gold oscilloscope lines crossing the wall, spiky, later smooth. Figures in a corner, lots of wall. Camera: slow push-ins, one close-up of the source. References: cinematographer Danny Cohen, designer Eve Stewart (*The King's Speech*, 2010). Avoid: centred symmetry; coarse dabbed strokes; map-like peel; neon-bright waves.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Digital oil brushwork, layered peeling painted walls, 1930s broadcast room, thin golden sound-wave lines

### Director's notes

Clawd plays George VI: a small crown, a wing collar and a dark tie, holding a cigarette in the consulting room and a typed script in the broadcast room. Three hard-cut shots tell the whole treatment: cowering in a corner, sound waves going from stammer to fluent, then Logue beating time as the speech goes out on the air.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Wide shot: Logue's consulting room, Clawd hunched at the left end of an old leather sofa holding a cigarette, a large expanse of peeling wall above and a horn gramophone on the right; slow push-in. (0.8s)
2. Big close-up: a marble microphone in a spring suspension ring, with Clawd's crown and eyes at left; the golden sound waves are broken at first (the stammer), then become smooth; slow push-in. (0.9s)
3. Medium wide shot: the 1939 broadcast room, Clawd at left with the script, the microphone in the center, Logue at right beating time with his hand, the ON AIR light coming on; slight pull-back. (0.9s)
