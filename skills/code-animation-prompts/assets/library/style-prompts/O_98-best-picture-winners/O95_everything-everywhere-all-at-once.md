# O95 Everything Everywhere All at Once

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Here and Now 2020–2025
- Looks like: Four universes, four looks, all real-time ray marching: the cold green IRS office, a hot dog finger piano under commercial lighting, a silent canyon with googly-eyed stones, an everything bagel turned black hole. As in the film, the jumps between universes are made with a googly-eye sticker.
- Style: ray-marched multiverse jump cuts
- Keywords: 3D
- Year: 2022

## Style prompt

Ray-marched real-time 3D multiverse montage: the subject jumps through 3–4 universes, each its own look, invented from its world, e.g. a realistic workplace under cold green fluorescents, densely dressed; a surreal cosmic version with a gravitational-lens halo; a silent wide landscape with white subtitles; a candy-pink product shot with pink-and-cyan rims. Each holds 2–4 s with its own grade. One link through all: a googly-eye sticker on the subject, about 1/10 of frame height. Transition, 0.5–0.8 s: push into the eye, smear, split RGB, then a rainbow ring of colour segments (#e5322d, #f7c928, #1fa39a, #2a6fc0, #e0478f) studded with googly eyes and pink beads blooms to ~80% of frame height and bursts to white. Signature frame: the ring around the subject; first jump at ~1 s. Reference: Daniels, *Everything Everywhere All at Once* (2022). Avoid: look-alike universes, a small plain ring.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

GLSL ray-marched multiple universes, one look per universe (realistic office, surreal universe, landscape, advertising), googly-eye rainbow mandala

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Evelyn, with a googly eye on her forehead and a fanny pack at her waist. Every universe jump first pushes in on the eye, then follows with motion trails, RGB split and a white flash, locked to the cut point; each universe has a different action: standing, pressing piano keys with the feet, sitting with the stone, being sucked into the bagel. At the end it returns to the start and opens its arms inside the mandala.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Wide shot of the IRS office, receipts flying everywhere, the camera pushing down the aisle toward Clawd; she has a googly eye on her forehead and a fanny pack at her waist. (0.8s)
2. Cut to the auditor and her steel desk piled with receipts, as she holds up a receipt; slow push-in. (0.6s)
3. Cut to Clawd with arms raised, the camera lunging in on the googly eye on her forehead as the universe-jump flash bursts. (0.4s)
4. The hot dog finger universe: a wide shot of a glossy piano under candy-pink light, one hand's hot dog fingers lying limp on the keys, Clawd standing on the keys. (0.5s)
5. Cut in close to Clawd's feet stamping the keys one after another, small hot dogs growing at the ends of both arms too. (0.5s)
6. Another push-in on the googly eye, leading into the next universe jump. (0.4s)
7. Wide shot at the edge of the silent canyon: two stones with googly eyes stuck on, Clawd sitting quietly beside them, the caption "We're all small and stupid."; the camera slowly drifts along the cliff edge. (1s)
8. The everything bagel: Clawd looks up at the giant black bagel, the camera pushing all the way into the black hole at its center and through the event horizon as Clawd is pulled forward, leaning in. (1.7s)
9. Back in the office: Clawd opens her arms, a ring of rainbow mandala with googly eyes growing behind her; the camera gently pulls back. (0.6s)
