## Style prompt


---

English:

Photoreal-leaning 3D landscape at dusk. Sky fills 60% of the frame, dusty ochre #D8A060 at the horizon up to pale cream, thin streaked clouds lit from below. Sun 1–2° above the horizon, still casting shadows; its light burns along the far ridge. Low saturation: hundreds of grey-green scrub clumps receding to the horizon, stones, dry grass, dark warm earth #2A2622 (default arid; elsewhere keep sparse clumped growth). The hero stands backlit in the mid-ground, about a third of frame height, long shadow toward the lens. Expose for the sky: the sun may clip, its glow within a tenth of the frame. Grade: neutral tone mapping, warm highlights, fine grain, vignette. Signature shot: locked, low, wide (~35 mm). Reference: cinematographer Roger Deakins (*No Country for Old Men*, 2007). Avoid: saturated orange sunsets; blown-out glare; bare ground; busy camera moves.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Real-time 3D (three.js), Roger Deakins' dusk light, low-saturation film grade

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Llewelyn: a light brown felt cowboy hat, a leather case. Three fixed-camera hard cuts: walking with the case at sunset, in the middle the "call it" coin, and finally headlights pressing in at night as the danger arrives. The light carries time from dusk to dark.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Low-angle backlit wide shot: Clawd as Llewelyn, in a cowboy hat and carrying the money case, walks over a small rise with the sun pressing on the horizon to his left and a shot-up pickup behind him; locked-off camera. (0.8s)
2. Macro: a quarter spinning on a gas station counter, with Chigurh's belt and hand out of focus behind it; the coin wobbles faster and faster, then falls; locked-off camera. No Clawd. (0.8s)
3. Night, low camera position close to an empty highway: Clawd stands on the shoulder as a pickup's headlights bear down from the crest of the hill; locked-off camera. (0.8s)
