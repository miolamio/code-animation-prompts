## Style prompt


---

English:

A letterpress poster from wood type and cuts. Three inks on cream stock #EEE3C7: blue #20488C (sky, water, shadow masses), red #C82C28 (warm surfaces, stripes), black #1E1B1E (outlines, type); only the hero the story centres on gets a spot ink (e.g. #D97757). Each ink 2-4 px off register; solids knock out the inks beneath, halftones overprint. Wood grain, pinholed starved patches, squeezed edges. Mid tones: coarse dots (about 1/200 of frame width), varied angles, on at most a third of the frame. Figures as linocuts: heavy outlines, gouged white lines, crescent shadows. Picture fills about 70%; short wood-type lines (about 1/10 of frame height, user's language) with offset shadows sit on banners and signs. Camera: small pushes, a lateral track, a bump on each strong beat (or every 2 s). Reference: Hatch Show Print. Avoid: busy grey mid-tones, perfect registration.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

wood-type letterpress printing, Hatch Show Print, linocut figures, halftone-dot portraits

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Willie Stark: a fedora pushed back, a loosened red-and-blue tie, a rolled-up speech in one hand. In the wide shot a fist is half raised; the middle shot is the crowd's response; in the close shot Clawd throws a fist all the way up on the beat and swings it again, the camera jolting lightly with the punch. All hard cuts.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Wide shot of a small-town square with a rostrum hung with bunting: Clawd as Stark stands before a giant halftone portrait of himself, one fist half raised, the crowd below cheering and waving STARK FOR GOVERNOR signs; the camera pushes in slightly. (0.9s)
2. Cut into the crowd: signs swinging up and down, fists and shouting mouths, EVERY MAN A KING; the camera moves laterally to the right. (0.7s)
3. Close shot of Clawd, fedora pushed back, tie loosened: the fist shoots straight to the top, then swings again; the camera jolts with the punch. (0.9s)
