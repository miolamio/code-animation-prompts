# O98 One Battle After Another

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Here and Now 2020–2025
- Looks like: A real-time 3D desert highway that rises and falls like a roller coaster, three cars chasing each other over ridge after ridge, shot with an extreme telephoto, with heat haze, bleached glare and fine grain. It recreates the film's most famous sequence: the rolling-highway car chase.
- Style: 3D rolling-highway car chase · telephoto compression
- Keywords: 3D
- Year: 2025

## Style prompt


---

English:

Real-time 3D, bleached desert daylight. Signature shot (3–5 s): a straight road over rolling dips and crests at a 2–5° horizontal field of view, stacking dips into waves; the travelling subject sinks and reappears; sky a thin band at most. Then 1–2 s each, a low camera at a crest, a mount on the vehicle or rider. Palette: sky #7fb0e0, haze #ece4d2 only at the far horizon, pale sand #d2ae80 (never orange), hard sun #fff0d8 from high to one side, the main vehicle faded seafoam #86b5ad. Contrast: dark cracked asphalt, crisp shadows, dense green scrub, roadside posts. Default midday; at other hours keep hard sun. Heat shimmer and mirage grow with distance; dust on landings; vehicles leave the ground over crests. Grain ±2%. Reference: Paul Thomas Anderson, *One Battle After Another* (2025). Avoid: wide lenses, a washed-out frame.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Real-time 3D (three.js), extreme telephoto compression, heat haze and road mirages, VistaVision fine grain

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Bob: the lapels of a plaid bathrobe, gold-framed sunglasses pushed up on the head, driving the lead car. The chase runs at real speed, with five camera positions alternating between the telephoto waves and close-in moments; the last shot rests on Clawd's raised arm, a pose held so the finale can pick it up.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Extreme telephoto looking back down the highway from in front of the convoy: the rolling terrain flattened into layered waves, three cars sinking into the waves and surfacing again, heat shimmer on the asphalt. (1.2s)
2. Cut to a hood-mounted position: Clawd in the driver's seat of a sea-foam green convertible, sunglasses pushed up on his head, both hands tight on the wheel, then glancing up at the rearview mirror. (0.8s)
3. Cut to a low tripod position on the shoulder at the crest of a hill: the convertible flies over the crest toward the camera. (0.6s)
4. Back to the telephoto, this time behind the convoy looking forward, the cars vanishing and reappearing in the successive undulations. (1.1s)
5. Cut to a telephoto tracking shot in front of the car: Clawd's convertible goes airborne over the crest, a dark green sedan and a grey sedan catching up from behind; after landing, Clawd stretches one hand out of the car and raises it, holding it up until the last frame. (1.3s)
