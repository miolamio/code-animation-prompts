# O5 Grand Hotel

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Silent and Early Sound 1927–1938
- Looks like: a circular lobby built in three.js, graded in MGM's silver black-and-white, with Clawd's orange as the only color. The film is best known for the shot looking straight down from above, the balconies closing in ring after ring, and the whole set is built around that one shot.
- Style: MGM silver black-and-white 3D lobby
- Keywords: 3D·B&W
- Year: 1931/32

## Style prompt

A real 3D set, by default an Art Deco interior (marble, chrome, chandelier, glossy checkerboard floor); outdoors keep the real setting with glossy surfaces (wet pavement, still water) and symmetrical framing. Ambient occlusion, soft bloom; 1930s studio silver: about 40% deep black, glossy whites near 90%, no washed-out greys. Light falls in shafts from one high source. All neutral grey except the hero the story centres on (e.g. #D97757), at least 1/6 of frame height; warm lamps render white, bloom only around them. Grain (±3%), halation. Signature shot: eye-height wide into a deep symmetrical set, floor and ceiling in frame, floor reflections; then 4-6 s tracks; one overhead on rings, figures as chess pieces. Period figures (heads, hats, limbs) walk routes. References: art director Cedric Gibbons, cinematographer William Daniels. Avoid: walking in place, frame-filling abstract patterns, colour casts.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

three.js 3D, MGM silver black-and-white, Art Deco interior, overhead concentric composition

### Director's notes

Clawd plays a bellhop in a small cylindrical cap, carrying a leather suitcase covered in hotel labels. He is really walking: 1.4 meters per second, 2.4 steps per second, with stride and displacement matching. The whole piece plays at 1:1, with a hard cut from the overhead shot to a side tracking shot at 1.08 s.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Looking straight down from beneath the skylight: six tiers of balconies closing in ring after ring toward the key tower, the orange bellhop the only dot on the checkerboard marble, walking across the floor; the camera pushes in slowly. (1.1s)
2. A side tracking shot at Clawd's height: he walks forward carrying the suitcase, the lobby sliding past behind him, the revolving door turning ahead; the camera follows and moves forward and in. (1.4s)

### Reference source

Edmund Goulding, 1932,Cedric Gibbons (art direction),William Daniels (cinematography)
