# O91 Green Book

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Indie Era 2010–2019
- Looks like: A "Greetings from the South" linen-texture postcard come to life: cloth-grain paper, saturated airbrush color, star-shaped glints on the chrome. The film is a 1962 road trip through the American South, on exactly the kind of roads shown on these postcards.
- Style: 1960s linen-texture postcard

## Style prompt


---

English:

A 1950s–60s linen postcard, Curt Teich manner. Linen-weave card: ink only on raised threads, a fine crosshatch about 1/300 of frame width, white specks in the hollows. Saturated hand-separated colour, airbrush blends: sky blue #3fa0dc, grass green #4fae57, pink #f08cb0, teal #2fb5b8 for the main object or water, cream card #f6e4b8 for paper and border. The main object large, side-on, about half the card's height. Star glints on shiny surfaces; registration off 1–2 px. Big navy 3D block letters with white outlines across the top third spell the place or title (invent one if needed), a small scene in each letter, a red ribbon banner above. Open on the card on a desk, push in until it fills the frame and comes alive (water, figures move), then track sideways. Avoid: weave-less gradients, greyish colour, photographic perspective.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Curt Teich linen-texture postcards, big "Greetings from" lettering, airbrushed hand coloring, offset print misregistration

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Tony: glossy black slicked-back hair, light blue short sleeves, a lit cigarette, one hand on the steering wheel. The push into the postcard carries straight into the road with no cut; the only cut lands in the concert hall, where Dr. Shirley plays the piano and Clawd watches from the wings.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. One continuous camera move: a postcard on a motel desk, with the Green Book and a room key beside it; the camera pushes all the way into the postcard, the picture comes alive, the teal Cadillac starts moving, and the camera pulls back and becomes a side tracking shot, Clawd at the wheel and Dr. Shirley in the back seat. (1.9s)
2. The single cut goes to the concert hall: wide shot, Dr. Shirley and his trio playing a grand piano before a red velvet curtain, Clawd standing in the wings watching; slight push-in. (0.6s)
