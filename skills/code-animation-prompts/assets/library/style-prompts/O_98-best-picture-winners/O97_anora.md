# O97 Anora

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Here and Now 2020–2025
- Looks like: Pink, teal and orange neon tubes glowing, their reflections dragging across a wet boardwalk and falling into drifting snow, with bloom, out-of-focus bokeh and grain. Anora runs from the Russian neon of Brighton Beach to the wedding signs of Vegas, all lit by tube light.
- Style: neon in a snowy night
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 2024

## Style prompt


---

English:

Photographic 2D neon night. Real tubes: glass tube, glow, bright tube, white-hot core, clips, dark jumper wires; rare flicker (every 5–10 s). Neon pink #ff4fa8, cyan #4fdcff, orange #ff9a2e, green #3dff9a plus warm sodium light over a deep purple-blue night #1e1838. Put neon where the subject's world allows (shopfronts, a harbour bar, its own building). Wet ground fills the lower quarter: signs stretch into long vertical reflections broken by plank gaps, paving joints or ripples; snow falls and drifts at the edges, tinted by the spill. The subject keeps a warm key on the face (a lamp, a window, its own light), narrow neon rims on both sides; the distance melts into bokeh. Bloom, vignette, grain ±3%. Short 2–3 s shots, new angles on one place, each a slow push-in. Reference: Sean Baker, *Anora* (2024). Avoid: flat neon lettering, strobing.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Neon tube signs, wet-ground reflections and snow cover, 35mm night photography

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Ani: a cream fur ushanka, a turned-up fur collar, one big hoop earring. Four short shots move on the beat, each with a little push or pull: fireworks, on the boardwalk with Vanya, the wedding sign, and finally the quiet car and the falling snow.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Telephoto shot of the lit Ferris wheel and parachute tower by the sea, a New Year firework bursting; slow push-in. (0.6s)
2. Cut to the snowy boardwalk with a row of Russian neon shop signs beside it: Clawd in the fur ushanka and fur collar stands in front, with Vanya laughing and waving next to him, the signs reflected in the wet planks; the camera slowly pulls back. (0.7s)
3. Cut to Las Vegas: a "Wedding" sign with a heart pierced by an arrow and a CHAPEL light box filling the frame, a small white chapel beside them; slow push-in. (0.6s)
4. Cut to the last scene, inside a parked car: Clawd sits on the dashboard as snow on the windshield behind him slides down and piles up, Igor in the foreground gripping the wheel; slow push-in. (0.6s)
