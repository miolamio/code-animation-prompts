# O73 Gladiator

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · New Millennium 2000–2009
- Looks like: The whole picture is laid in square stone tesserae and glass pieces: the stones along the outlines follow the form, flat areas are laid in straight rows, and the gold-leaf glass pieces glint. The film itself is set in Rome, so the Colosseum and the Elysian wheat field are both made as Roman floor mosaics.

## Style prompt


---

English:

Roman floor mosaic: over a flat underdrawing lay square tesserae about 1/150 of frame width: two to three courses follow each outline (opus vermiculatum), flat areas in straight rows, figures outlined with one course of dark stones. Muted, warm stone and glass: burnt gold #E2B24C, umber #5B4332, terracotta #8E3C2B, warm marble #E9DDC4, with imperial-purple glass for one accent only; sky and water in pale grey-blue and cream stone, never saturated bands. Tesserae slightly irregular, lit from the upper left, shadowing into dark 1–2 px grout; gold-leaf glass glints slowly on light sources. Frontal, slightly stiff Pompeian figures before a faded field of fine stones. Short pushes and pulls; motion from details (a gesture, a crowd, plants, a light shaft). References: *Alexander Mosaic*, Zliten mosaic. Avoid: a neat grid that reads as pixel art; oversized tiles; smooth gradients; bright grout.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Form-following tesserae (opus vermiculatum), gold-leaf glass inlay, Zliten gladiator mosaic

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Maximus: a bronze shoulder guard and a Roman short sword. It opens on the thumbs-down; hard cut to the Colosseum, with the crowd standing up around Clawd; the last cut leaves the fight for the quiet wheat field of Elysium. Three hard cuts, about one second each.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Close shot: the purple imperial box, Commodus's thumb turning from up to down; slight push-in. (0.8s)
2. Poster-like wide shot of the Colosseum: Clawd as Maximus stands in the center, short sword pointing down, a chained tiger on the left and Tigris on the right, spectators on the stands rising to their feet; slight pull-back. (1s)
3. Wide shot of Elysium: under a low sun Clawd stands in a golden wheat field, cypresses and a farmhouse on the horizon; slow push-in. (0.8s)
