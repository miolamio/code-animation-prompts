## Style prompt


---

English:

1920s stone lithograph poster in four inks: sea blue #245A8C, ochre #D49640, deep brown #40271B, warm red #E47E62, on aged cream #F1E7D1, misregistered about 1/700 of frame width. Every tone is ink grain, never a flat fill: specks about 1/1000 of frame width in soft clumps, covering 20–40% of pale areas, 50–80% of mid-tones, nearly all of solids (with pinholes); no stray specks on bare paper. Roles: blue for sky, water, cool shadow; ochre for ground and skin; brown for outlines, darks, clothes; red sparingly. Values calm and pale. Classical figures: ochre lit side, brown core-shadow line down each limb, head 1/8 of body; groups in a row. Slow motion carried by spray, dust, hair, cloth. Camera: long-lens front views, ground-level shots; optional serif title band. Reference: Jean Droit, 1924 Paris Olympics poster. Avoid: coarse speckle, pictogram figures, vector fills.

### Director's notes

Clawd runs in the pack: a white headband, bib number 98 pinned to the chest. The running is at 1x speed, which reads as the slow motion of the original film. Four hard cuts: faces, feet, Liddell's head-back running style, the whole team from the side; the last shot prints the poster's text frame over the picture, turning the scene into a 1924 poster.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Telephoto frontal view: the runners come toward the camera along the water's edge, Clawd among them wearing a white headband and bib number 98; the camera pulls back slightly. (0.7s)
2. Low close shot: bare feet land in shallow water and splash, reflections on the wet sand; the camera slides sideways. (0.6s)
3. Close shot of Liddell: head thrown back, mouth open, Abrahams behind him; the camera pulls back slightly. (0.5s)
4. The whole team of runners from the side, Clawd in the middle; as the camera pulls back, the poster text frame "JEUX DE LA VIIIe OLYMPIADE · PARIS 1924" prints onto the picture. (0.8s)
