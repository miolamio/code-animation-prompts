# O58 Out of Africa

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Blockbuster Era 1980–1989
- Looks like: Low-altitude real-time 3D flight: a chrome-yellow biplane skims a soda lake, tens of thousands of flamingos startle into the air, amber misty light. The most remembered moment of the film is Denys flying Karen over Kenya, and the hand she reaches back to the rear seat.
- Style: 3D biplane over the East African Rift
- Keywords: 3D
- Year: 1985

## Style prompt

Real-time 3D like 1980s widescreen location photography: true-scale models with fine detail (e.g. struts, wires, rivets); smooth terrain, not low-poly. Default light: late-afternoon sun low behind the hero as seen from camera, pale cream sky, amber haze, distant ridges in 3–4 paler layers, ochre ground with long soft shadows. Pale overall: about 70% of the frame in light mid-tones, shadows warm grey, not deep brown; under a fifth near-white. Grade: filmic curve, low saturation, light grain, soft vignette. Thousands of instanced birds or animals (e.g. pink #F3B3AE with crimson #DF3F55) lift or scatter in staggered waves as the hero passes. Signature shot: over the moving hero's shoulder across open land; then a high chase 10–20 m up and one shot into the sun. Reference: cinematographer David Watkin (*Out of Africa*, 1985). Avoid: cold clean CG daylight; dark sepia; lockstep flocks.

### Director's notes

Clawd sits in Karen's front seat: a leather flying cap, brass goggles, a white scarf. Two exterior shots hard-cut together to establish speed and space over the flamingo lake; the final cockpit shot is saved for the film's signature gesture: Clawd turns and reaches back, Denys takes his hand, landing on the last few beats. Flight time runs 1.5-1.6x screen time.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Low-angle chase shot skimming the lake surface: the yellow biplane sweeps overhead, Clawd small in the front seat leaning out to look, flamingos lifting off the water and passing the camera. (0.8s)
2. Hard cut to a wide backlit shot facing the sun: the plane flies toward the camera, flanked by flocks of pink birds rising. (0.7s)
3. In-cockpit shot from the rear seat looking forward: Clawd, in a leather flying cap and brass goggles with a white scarf fluttering in the wind, turns and reaches back, and Denys's gloved hand takes it. (1s)
