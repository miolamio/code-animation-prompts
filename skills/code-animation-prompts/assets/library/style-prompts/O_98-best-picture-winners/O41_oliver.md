## Style prompt

Victorian toy paper theatre (Pollock's), all printed card: black lithographic outlines about 1/400 of frame width, sparse crayon hatching on one side of each piece; muted antique hand colouring: deep red #C8262C and dull gold #E0AB36 on proscenium and curtains (never bright yellow), blue #2F5A9A for costumes; scenery, sky, water and skin in greyed browns, greys and cream card #F4ECD6. Every cut-out keeps a white scissor margin (1/200 of frame width). Flats are parallel 2.5D layers in parallax, flat within each plane. Candle footlights throw each cut-out's shadow onto the scenery behind, about 1.5× enlarged, soft, flickering. Gilt proscenium, red drapes and painted audience boxes frame a stage opening 80–90% of frame width. Camera: slow push, lateral tracking, shaky close-ups; a final pull-back shows the dark room. Avoid: bright vector colour, true 3D depth, soft studio light, figures without margins.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

Pollock's toy theatre, hand-colored lithographed paper figures, cut cardboard and footlight shadows, Cruikshank caricature figures

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Oliver: a paper figure stuck on its own sliding rod, holding up an empty bowl. Three shot sizes (wide, stage, close) build up the layers: it steps forward, raises the empty bowl on the beat, Bumble roars, it shrinks back, and the bowl stays raised.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Wide shot: the toy theatre on a mahogany table in a dark room, a "Theatre Royal" proscenium, a cut-out figure on the table and a puppeteer's hand reaching in beside it; slow push-in. (0.5s)
2. Lateral move along the stage, the workhouse dining hall: Clawd (Oliver) steps out of a row of children and raises the empty bowl (Please, sir...), beside a steaming pot of gruel, Bumble's brow furrowing; the camera follows it to the right. (0.9s)
3. Close shot of Clawd and Bumble: Bumble bellows, Clawd flinches and trembles, the bowl still held up; the camera jolts lightly at the bellow. (1.1s)
