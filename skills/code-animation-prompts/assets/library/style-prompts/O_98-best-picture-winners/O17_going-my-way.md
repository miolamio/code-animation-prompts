# O17 Going My Way

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Color and War 1939–1949

## Style prompt

A backlit Gothic stained-glass window filling 80-90% of frame height and ~60% of width, neighbours glowing at edges. Every shape is cut glass: lightness varied ±8% per piece, streaks, seed bubbles, a brown edge mat, black lead (about 1/250 of frame width). Sky and empty ground: diamond quarries (about 1/30 of frame width); the border is ruby with beads. The main figure is at least half the window's height, in dark-brown grisaille line. Palette by role: background cobalt #1B3F9E, border ruby #B3121F, highlights gold #E3A526, lead #16161A; skin pale amber; scenery deep greens. Only the hero the story centres on is orange glass (e.g. #D97757). Bright glass blooms into the leads; coloured light spills onto floor and sill. Camera: tilt down, pan, settle on the pooled light. References: Chartres Cathedral; Harry Clarke. Scenery also uses teal-green. Avoid: flat fills, random cuts, dark empty wall.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

lead-came colored glass + grisaille painting, Chartres-blue diamond lattice, Harry Clarke jewel tones

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Father O'Malley in the glass painting: its body is the warmest terracotta-colored glass, with a straw hat and a Roman collar, holding a baton and a songbook. It conducts to the beat while the boys' mouths move out of step with each other. A tilt down runs from the star medallion to Clawd, then a hard cut to the choir, and finally down to the colored light on the floor. Performance at 1.8x speed.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Starting from the golden octagonal star medallion and the "SWINGING ON A STAR" banner, tilt down along the pointed-arch window to Clawd as Father O'Malley, who conducts the boys' choir within the glass. (1.3s)
2. Medium shot of the glass choir: boys in red robes and white surplices singing, the camera panning right past Clawd's hand raising the baton. (0.6s)
3. Cut to the church nave: the colors of the stained-glass windows spilling across the marble aisle between the pews, the altar candles lit; the camera tilts down to the floor. (0.6s)
