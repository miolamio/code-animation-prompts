## Notes

### Technique keywords

cyanotype blueprint, general arrangement of a timber trestle bridge, red-pencil revisions, gouache-colored protagonist

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Colonel Nicholson: a British Army peaked cap and a swagger stick, standing on his own bridge whistling. Three shots tell cause and effect: the train rolls onto the bridge, the plunger handle goes down, the bridge span blows apart. The explosion stays on the drawing too: red-pencil scribbles make the fireball and hatching draws the smoke, with no realistic fire glow.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Medium-wide shot of the bridge elevation: Clawd (Colonel Nicholson) stands on the bridge deck whistling, musical notes drifting upward, a column of prisoners marching at lower left, the plunger at right, and a train coming onto the bridge from the far right; slow push-in. (1s)
2. Close-up of the plunger box (PLUNGER - ONE PUSH): the T-handle goes down, a red-pencil fuse line running to the explosives; slight push-in. (0.5s)
3. Back to the bridge elevation: the charges go off, a fireball scribbled in red pencil wrapped in a hatched cloud of smoke, the bridge span and train cars dropping into the river; slow pull back. (1s)
