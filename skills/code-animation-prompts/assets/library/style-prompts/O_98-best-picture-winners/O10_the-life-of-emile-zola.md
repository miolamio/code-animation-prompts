# O10 The Life of Emile Zola

- Category: O 98 Best Picture Winners · Silent and Early Sound 1927–1938
- Looks like: newspaper letterpress printing combined with 1890s line-engraved illustrations: wood-type headlines, densely set metal-type body text, ink spread and dropout on yellowed newsprint, and figures drawn in hatching. The film's most famous image is the front page of *L'Aurore* on 13 January 1898 with "J'Accuse...!".
- Style: newspaper letterpress + line-engraved illustration
- Keywords: 2D·B&W
- Year: 1937

## Style prompt

1890s newspaper print. Set text as a sharp mask, then print: ~1 px blur and a noisy threshold for ink spread, ragged edges, under-inked patches, faint mirrored show-through. Page grammar: masthead, a wood-type headline across the width (~1/6 of frame height), 3-6 columns of small type in the user's language (~1/80 of frame height) flanking one large engraved picture. Wood-type letters are grained blocks. Everything pictured is a line engraving: hatching lines about 1/1000 of frame width, spaced 1/400, following the form, cross-hatching only in darks, heavier shadow-side contours, paler distance. Warm black #17130F on newsprint #E8E0CC; the hero the story centres on gets one accent plate (e.g. #D97757), offset 1-2 px. Motion: type blocks slam down, bounce and leave their print; one repeating mechanical motion, pages turning. Avoid: clean digital type, smooth grey shading, other colours.

## Notes

### Technique keywords

wood-type headline, newsprint letterpress, 1890s line engraving

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Zola: pince-nez, quill pen, holding that day's newspaper. On beat 2 the last letter lands and the whole page jolts; on beat 3 he looks up from the paper with the quill raised high. The scene runs at 1.05x with two hard cuts: headline → Zola → printing press.

### Storyboard in the final film

1. Close-up of the front page of *L'Aurore* (JEUDI 13 JANVIER 1898): the wood-type letters of "J'Accuse...!" slam down one by one, and as the last one lands the whole page jolts; the camera pushes in. (0.9s)
2. Medium shot: Clawd as Zola stands before the front page, looks up from the newspaper in his hand, hops, and raises the quill high, a newsboy hawking papers at right; gentle pull back. (0.8s)
3. The rotary press turning, a printer holding up a wet newspaper, copies flying out one after another; the camera pulls back and moves right. (0.8s)
